# Recruiting operators for the Sathi pilot

Messages and a demo script for signing up 10 operators in each pilot district (see [README.md](README.md)). Copy a message, fill in the blanks (`[...]`), and send it. Keep it short and personal: these work best from your own phone after a first call.

Order of work:

1. Message the **district CSC manager** (or e-governance society) and ask for introductions (section 1).
2. Message or visit the **operators** they suggest, plus kiosks you find near the tehsil office (section 2).
3. Give each interested operator the **5-minute demo** at their own kiosk (section 3).
4. Send the **welcome message** to everyone who agrees (section 4), and add them to the district WhatsApp group.

Keep operator names and phone numbers in your own sheet. Do not commit them to this repository.

## 3. The 5-minute demo (at the operator's kiosk)

Bring two printed handouts. Do the demo on **the operator's own computer or phone**, so they know it works there. If a real customer with a stuck case walks in, use them instead of the examples below (with the customer's permission).

| Minute | You do | You say (gist) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0–1 | Ask, then listen | "How many people come in each week whose money hasn't arrived or whose file is stuck? What do you tell them now?" |
| 1–2 | Scan the QR code on the handout; switch the language | "This is free and needs no login. It opens straight to the status checker." |
| 2–3 | Pick **PM-KISAN** (UP: *PM-KISAN (UP)*); paste the example status `eKYC Status: No` → **Find my stage** | "It recognises the line from the portal and says what it means and what to do next, in [Hindi / Marathi]." |
| 3–4 | Paste `Payment Failed: Aadhaar not seeded`; set an application date 60 days ago; open the complaint letter | "When the case is stuck or overdue, it writes the complaint to the right officer and says where to submit it. You only type the name and mobile, never Aadhaar or OTP." |
| 4–5 | Show page 2 of the handout | "For 4 weeks, write one line per customer. No names. I'll collect a photo every Friday. ₹[1,500] when you finish. Will you try it?" |

More examples to paste if they ask:

| Scheme | Paste this | Sathi shows |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| PM-KISAN | `FTO is Generated and Payment confirmation is pending` | Payment order made; wait 1–2 weeks |
| PM-KISAN | `Land Seeding Status: No` | Land record not linked; meet the Talathi / Lekhpal |
| Old-age pension (UP) | `Application pending at BDO for verification` | Waiting for BDO / SDM verification |
| Old-age pension (UP) | `PFMS payment failed` | Sanctioned but not paid; fix the bank link, then complain |
| Ladki Bahin (MH) | the status line from the Ladki Bahin portal or SMS | Matching stage and next step |

### Questions operators ask

| Question | Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| Is this a government website? | No. It's an independent, free guide. Every guide links to the official portals and lists its sources. |
| Will it take away my income? | No. Customers still need you to do the portal work, eKYC and printing. Sathi makes you faster and the customer more likely to come back. Charging your normal fee for that work is fine; just don't charge for using Sathi itself. |
| What happens to the customer's data? | Nothing is stored or sent. What you type stays in your browser. The case log has no names or numbers. |
| What if Sathi is wrong? | Tap **Report an error** on the guide, or message the group. Errors are fixed within a week. |
| How much time does it take? | Usually 2–5 minutes per case. The log line takes 30 seconds. |
| Why are you doing this? | To learn where people get stuck and fix it. At the end, the district gets an anonymous summary. |
