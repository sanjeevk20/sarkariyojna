Sathi · Sathi pilot: operator guide

Customer's government work stuck? Find the next step in 2 minutes

Sathi is a free website that reads a scheme's status and tells the customer what it means, what to do next, and drafts a complaint if the office is late.

sanjeevk20.github.io/sarkariyojna/#check

Use it when a customer says

  • “My money / instalment has not come”
  • “My application has been pending for a long time”
  • “It shows rejected and I don't know why”

Status checker covers

PM-KISAN, Ladki Bahin, income certificate (UP), old-age pension (UP), Ladli Behna (MP), Gruha Lakshmi (Karnataka), social security pension (Rajasthan). Step-by-step guides cover 68 schemes; open any guide from the home page.

Six steps

  1. Scan the QR code or open sanjeevk20.github.io/sarkariyojna/#check. Choose the language.
  2. Choose the scheme.
  3. Paste the status text from the portal or SMS, or tick the status that matches. Enter the date of application.
  4. Read out What it means and What to do next to the customer.
  5. If it is overdue or stuck: type only the customer's name and mobile, then print, copy or WhatsApp the complaint letter, and tell them where to submit it.
  6. Write one line in the case log (page 2). Set a follow-up date 30 days ahead.

Rules

  • Sathi is free. Do not charge for using it. Your normal fee for portal work is fine.
  • Never type Aadhaar number, bank account, OTP or passwords into Sathi. It does not need them.
  • What you type stays in this browser. Nothing is sent unless the customer chooses Help others: share this anonymously.
  • Do not write customer names, Aadhaar or mobile numbers in the case log.

What to tell the customer

“This is what your status means. Do this next. If nothing changes by [follow-up date], come back and we will send this complaint.”
