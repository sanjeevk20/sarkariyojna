# Sathi: one page

**MyScheme tells you what you're eligible for. Haqdarshak helps you apply. Sathi gets you unstuck.**

## The problem

Millions of applications for pensions, PM-KISAN instalments, women's monthly payments, certificates and land mutations sit "pending", "failed" or "rejected" with no explanation the citizen can act on. People pay agents, make repeated trips to the office, or give up. The village kiosk operator they ask is guessing too.

## What Sathi does

For one scheme at a time, Sathi tells a citizen:

1. what their status means;
2. the exact next step;
3. what the law says the office owes them, and when.

When the time limit has passed, it drafts the complaint letter and says where to send it. All of this is in the citizen's own language, with the official source shown.

## The pilot (next 8 weeks)

- **Who and where:** 20 CSC / kiosk operators. Ten are in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra, and ten in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh.
- **How long:** 4 weeks of use, then follow-up calls 30 days later to see what happened to each case.
- **Data:** a paper case log with no personal data.
- **Pass marks:**
  - 300 cases logged;
  - a status stage identified in 80% of cases;
  - 40% of stuck files solved within 30 days;
  - 10 minutes or less per case;
  - 70% of operators want to keep using it.
- **Cost:** about ₹60,000.

## Why operators and states should care

- **Operators** sort out a stuck case faster, and the customer comes back.
- **District administrations** get a monthly anonymous count of where cases stall, by scheme and stage. That is a cheap early warning for grievance cells.
- **States** see fewer repeat visits and fewer complaints escalating to CM helplines.

## How it can sustain itself

1. Paid dashboards and alerts for district administrations and CSR programmes.
2. Training and white-label versions for kiosk networks.
3. Grants and work orders from state innovation programmes.

The citizen side stays free.
