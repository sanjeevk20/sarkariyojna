Ayushman Bharat Card: How to make it for free

आयुष्मान भारत कार्ड: मोफत कसे काढायचे
Free health card for cashless treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year, anywhere in India
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Card in hand
Make the card
1
You
Check if your family is eligible पात्रता तपासा
Check on beneficiary.nha.gov.in or the Ayushman app ("Am I eligible") using your mobile number, ration card or Aadhaar.
  • Eligible poor and vulnerable families
  • In Maharashtra, families covered under MJPJAY
  • Everyone aged 70 or more, whatever their income (Ayushman Vay Vandana card, up to ₹5 lakh a year for the senior)
2
You
Keep these ready हे तयार ठेवा
  • Aadhaar of each family member
  • Mobile number linked to Aadhaar (for OTP)
  • Ration card
  • A smartphone, or go to a CSC / hospital help desk
Stuck? Mobile not linked to Aadhaar? Use face authentication in the app, or update the mobile at an Aadhaar centre.
3
You
Make the card (it is free) कार्ड काढा (मोफत)
On the Ayushman app: log in with mobile → search your family → select a member → Aadhaar eKYC (OTP or face) → take photo → submit.
Or get help at a CSC / e-Seva Kendra, the Ayushman Mitra desk at a network hospital, or your ASHA worker.
Tip: Repeat for every family member. Each person gets their own card.
After you apply
4
Government
Card is approved कार्ड मंजूर होते
The State Health Agency checks the details and approves the card. This is usually quick.
Stuck? Status pending for long or rejected? Often the name on Aadhaar doesn't match the family list. Ask the Ayushman Mitra or CSC to correct it.
5
You
Download your card कार्ड डाउनलोड करा
Download the card (PDF) from the app or website. Keep it on your phone and print a copy.
6
Done
Use it at any network hospital in India भारतातील कोणत्याही नेटवर्क रुग्णालयात वापरा
At an empanelled hospital, show your card or Aadhaar at the Ayushman Mitra desk. Covered treatment is cashless, even in another state.
Stuck? Asked to pay for covered treatment? Refuse politely, ask for it in writing, and call the Ayushman helpline.
Card or treatment problem? Complain.
Use the grievance option on the Ayushman portal / app, call the Ayushman helpline, or contact your district's Ayushman / MJPJAY coordinator.
Card: Ayushman app / beneficiary.nha.gov.in · Hospitals: hospitals.pmjay.gov.in
The card is free, never pay anyone to make it · Use only .gov.in websites and official apps
