Mahatma Phule Mahamandal: Business loan and subsidy for SC families

महात्मा फुले मागासवर्गीय विकास महामंडळ: अनुसूचित जातीतील कुटुंबांसाठी व्यवसाय कर्ज व अनुदान
Subsidy, low-interest margin money and loans to start a small business
You do thisCorporation / bank does this (you wait)Business running
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
  • From a Scheduled Caste or Nav-Bauddha family, living in Maharashtra
  • Within the age limit (adult, working age)
  • Family income below the scheme limit
  • Not a defaulter on any earlier government / corporation loan
2
You
Pick the right scheme योग्य योजना निवडा
  • Small project → subsidy scheme: part subsidy, rest bank loan
  • Medium project → seed capital (margin money): corporation gives a low-interest share, bank gives the main loan
  • Larger project → NSFDC term loan through the corporation at low interest
Tip: Ask the district office which scheme fits your project amount. Limits change by GR.
3
You
Plan the business, get quotations व्यवसायाची योजना, कोटेशन घ्या
Decide the business (shop, dairy, auto-rickshaw, tailoring, workshop, etc.). Get quotations for machines / goods and make a simple project report.
4
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Caste certificate
  • Income certificate, domicile, age proof
  • Aadhaar, ration card, photo, bank passbook
  • Project report and quotations
  • Shop / place proof (rent agreement or ownership), if needed
  • No-dues certificate from banks, if asked
Stuck? No caste or income certificate? Get them first on Aaple Sarkar.
5
You
Apply at the district office जिल्हा कार्यालयात अर्ज करा
Submit the form with documents at the corporation's district office (or online where available). Take an acknowledgement with the application number.
Tip: Applying is free. Never pay an agent to "get the loan sanctioned".
After you apply
6
Corporation / Bank
Scrutiny, site visit and sanction छाननी, स्थळ पाहणी व मंजुरी
The district office checks your papers and may visit your proposed business place. The application is placed before the sanction committee.
Stuck? Pending for long? Ask the district manager for the status in writing. Missing quotations or place proof are common reasons.
7
Corporation / Bank
Money is released रक्कम वितरित होते
For subsidy / seed-capital schemes the file goes to the bank, which sanctions its loan. Money is usually paid to the supplier for the asset. The office may verify the asset was bought.
Stuck? Bank delaying its share? Contact the corporation's district office; they follow up with the bank.
8
Done
Run the business, repay on time व्यवसाय चालवा, हप्ते वेळेवर भरा
Start your business. Pay every EMI on time to the bank / corporation. Good repayment makes you eligible for bigger loans later.
Stuck? Missed EMIs make you a defaulter and block future loans and schemes.
Stuck for long? Escalate.
Give a written application to the corporation's district manager with your application number and take an acknowledgement (पोच). If not solved, write to the regional / head office. For bank delays, contact the district Lead Bank Manager.
Apply at: Mahatma Phule Mahamandal district office · Website: mpbcdc.maharashtra.gov.in · Keep all receipts and EMI records
