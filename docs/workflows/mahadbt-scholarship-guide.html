MahaDBT Scholarship: How to get it step by step

महाडीबीटी शिष्यवृत्ती: पायरी-पायरीने कशी मिळवायची
Fee and maintenance support for college students, paid into your bank account
You do thisCollege / Government does this (you wait)Money received
Before you apply
1
You
Check which scheme fits you कोणती योजना लागू ते तपासा
Schemes depend on your category, family income and course. After you fill your profile, MahaDBT shows the schemes you are eligible for.
Tip: You can get only one scholarship for the same course. Pick the one that gives you the most.
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Aadhaar, linked to your bank account
  • Income certificate of the current year
  • Caste certificate (and caste validity, if your course needs it)
  • Domicile certificate
  • Last exam marksheet, admission / fee receipt
  • Gap certificate, if you had a gap year
Stuck? Income certificate from last year? Get a new one first. Not sure your bank is Aadhaar-linked? Ask the bank for "Aadhaar seeding / NPCI mapping".
3
You
Register and fill your profile नोंदणी करून प्रोफाइल भरा
Go to mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in → New Applicant Registration. Fill every section of the profile (personal, income, caste, course, past education, hostel) until it is 100% complete.
Tip: Note down your username and password. You need them every year for renewal.
4
You
Apply before the deadline अंतिम तारखेपूर्वी अर्ज करा
Choose your scheme, upload documents and submit. Note your Application ID.
Tip: Don't wait for the last week. The portal gets slow and deadlines are strict.
After you apply
5
College / Government
College checks your application महाविद्यालयाकडून तपासणी
Your college's scholarship desk checks your documents and forwards the application.
Stuck? Status says sent back? Read the remark, fix it and resubmit. Pending at college for long? Visit the scholarship desk with your Application ID.
6
College / Government
Department approves विभागाची मंजुरी
The welfare department for your scheme (e.g. Social Justice, OBC, Tribal, Higher Education) checks and sanctions the scholarship.
7
Done
Money in your bank account पैसे बँक खात्यात जमा
The amount is paid by DBT into your Aadhaar-linked bank account. For some schemes, the fee part goes directly to the college.
Stuck? Approved but no money? Your bank account may not be Aadhaar-seeded or may be inactive. Fix it at the bank.
Pending too long? Escalate.
If the college or department has not acted for a long time, raise a grievance on the MahaDBT portal with your Application ID, or contact the district office of your scheme's department.
Renew every year on MahaDBT: you need last year's pass result and a fresh income certificate
Check status: MahaDBT login → My Applied Schemes · Use only .gov.in websites
