PM-KISAN + CM Kisan Kalyan: ₹10,000 a year for MP farmers

पीएम-किसान + मुख्यमंत्री किसान कल्याण: मप्र के किसानों को वर्ष में ₹10,000
₹6,000 a year from PM-KISAN plus ₹4,000 a year from the state (as reported)
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Money received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
Your name must be in the khasra / B-1 as a landholder. You cannot get it if someone in the family pays income tax, holds a government job, or is a practising doctor, lawyer, CA or engineer.
Stuck? Land still in a parent's name? Get naamantaran done first. See the Land mutation (MP) guide.
2
You
Register for PM-KISAN पीएम-किसान में पंजीकरण करें
Register on pmkisan.gov.in or at a CSC with khasra, Aadhaar, bank passbook and mobile. No separate form is needed for CM Kisan Kalyan: approved PM-KISAN farmers are included automatically.
3
Government
Land verification भूमि सत्यापन
The Patwari / tehsil checks your name against the land record and the state approves you.
Stuck? Name spelling differs between Aadhaar, khasra and bank? Correct it, then ask the Patwari to re-verify.
After you apply
4
You
Do eKYC ई-केवाईसी करें
Complete eKYC by OTP on pmkisan.gov.in, face authentication in the PM-KISAN app, or fingerprint at a CSC.
Stuck? Without eKYC, both the central and state payments stop.
5
Government
Payments released भुगतान जारी
PM-KISAN pays ₹2,000 three times a year; the state pays its ₹4,000 a year in instalments into the same account.
6
Done
Money in your account खाते में राशि
Check PM-KISAN on pmkisan.gov.in and the state instalments on saara.mp.gov.in.
Stuck? PM-KISAN received but not the state amount? Check saara.mp.gov.in, then contact the tehsil / agriculture office.
Instalment missing? Complain.
Use the PM-KISAN helpline 155261 / 011-24300606, the district agriculture office, or the CM Helpline 181.
