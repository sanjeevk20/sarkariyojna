Social Security Pension (MP): Old-age, widow and disability pension

सामाजिक सुरक्षा पेंशन (मध्य प्रदेश): वृद्धावस्था, कल्याणी और दिव्यांग पेंशन
About ₹600 a month by DBT (higher for some 80+ beneficiaries, as reported)
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Pension received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
  • Old-age: 60 or older
  • Kalyani (widow) pension: widowed women
  • Disability pension: with a disability certificate
  • Low-income family, as per the scheme rules
2
You
Keep these ready ये तैयार रखें
  • Samagra member ID with eKYC
  • Aadhaar, age proof
  • Widows: husband's death certificate
  • Bank account linked to Samagra and Aadhaar
Tip: Almost every MP service needs a Samagra member ID with eKYC done. No Samagra yet? See the Samagra ID (MP) guide first.
3
You
Apply आवेदन करें
Apply on socialsecurity.mp.gov.in, at the Gram Panchayat / Janpad, or at the urban body office. Keep the application number.
Tip: Applying is free. Under the Lok Seva Guarantee Act a decision is due within a fixed time (15 working days reported).
After you apply
4
Government
Verification and sanction सत्यापन और स्वीकृति
The Janpad / urban body verifies and sanctions the pension.
Stuck? No decision in time? File a first appeal or complain on the CM Helpline 181.
5
Done
Pension every month हर महीने पेंशन
Paid by DBT into the Samagra-linked account.
Stuck? Payment stopped? Samagra eKYC is now mandatory for payment; redo it, and check the bank account is active.
Stopped or not sanctioned? Complain.
Complain on the CM Helpline 181 or to the Janpad CEO / urban body with your application or Samagra number.
Apply / status: socialsecurity.mp.gov.in · Amounts as reported; check the portal
