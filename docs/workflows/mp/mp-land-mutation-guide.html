Naamantaran (MP): Get your name into the land record

नामांतरण (मध्य प्रदेश): भू-अभिलेख में अपना नाम दर्ज कराएं
Your name in the khasra / B-1 record, needed for loans, PM-KISAN, sale and compensation
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Name recorded
At registration
1
You
Register the deed on Sampada संपदा पर रजिस्ट्री कराएं
Register the sale or gift deed at the sub-registrar office (Sampada 2.0). Make sure khasra numbers and names match the land record.
Tip: If you buy a whole khasra with no dispute, Cyber Tehsil can do the naamantaran automatically, without a tehsil visit.
2
Government
Cyber Tehsil or tehsil case साइबर तहसील या तहसील प्रकरण
Sampada sends the deed to the revenue system (RCMS). Whole-khasra, undisputed sales go to Cyber Tehsil; part-sales, inheritance and disputed cases become a case before the Tehsildar.
After registration
3
Government
Notice for objections आपत्ति के लिए सूचना
A public notice gives others a chance to object before the order.
Stuck? Someone objected? The case goes to a hearing before the Tehsildar. Bring the deed and witnesses.
4
Government
Order passed आदेश पारित
Simple Cyber Tehsil cases are reported to finish in about 15 days; tehsil cases take longer.
Stuck? Nothing after a month? Check the case on RCMS with your registry number, then visit the tehsil.
5
Done
Your name in the record रिकॉर्ड में आपका नाम
Check the updated khasra / B-1 on the MP land records portal and download a copy.
Tip: Inheritance (fauti naamantaran) is always a tehsil case: apply with the death certificate and heirs' details.
Stuck for weeks? Escalate.
Naamantaran is a time-bound service. Ask the Tehsildar for the case status, file a first appeal if the limit is crossed, or complain on the CM Helpline 181.
Registration: Sampada 2.0 · Case status: RCMS · Records: MP land records portal
