Ladli Laxmi Yojana: Support for your daughter from school to 21

लाड़ली लक्ष्मी योजना: स्कूल से 21 वर्ष तक बेटी के लिए सहायता
An assurance certificate of about ₹1.43 lakh in the girl's name, paid in stages
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Instalment received
Register
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
  • Girl born on or after 1 January 2006 to an MP family
  • Birth registered (ideally within a year)
  • Parents not income-tax payers; family limits apply (usually up to two daughters)
2
You
Register the girl बेटी का पंजीकरण करें
Register on ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in, at your Anganwadi centre, or at a Lok Seva Kendra. The girl and the family need a Samagra ID with eKYC.
Tip: Registration is free. Keep the registration number and the printed assurance certificate safe.
3
Government
Approval and certificate स्वीकृति और प्रमाण पत्र
The Women and Child Development department checks the application and issues the assurance certificate in the girl's name.
Stuck? Pending for months? Ask the Anganwadi supervisor or the project office (CDPO) with the registration number.
At each stage
4
You
At each school stage, keep records updated हर स्कूल चरण पर रिकॉर्ड अपडेट रखें
Stage payments are linked to admission in Class 6, 9, 11 and 12. Make sure the school enters her admission and her bank account is ready.
Tip: Exact stage amounts differ between sources and changed under Ladli Laxmi 2.0. Check the current table on the portal.
5
Done
Stage money and final amount चरण राशि और अंतिम राशि
Stage amounts reach her account by DBT. The final amount is paid after age 21 if she is unmarried until 18 and meets the study conditions.
Stuck? Stage payment missing? Check that the school admission is recorded and Samagra eKYC is done; then contact the CDPO.
Payment stuck? Complain.
Contact the Anganwadi supervisor / CDPO, the women's helpline 181, or the CM Helpline 181 with the registration number.
Register / status: ladlilaxmi.mp.gov.in, Anganwadi, Lok Seva Kendra · Amounts and conditions as reported; check the portal
