Samagra ID: The family ID behind almost every MP scheme

समग्र आईडी: मध्य प्रदेश की लगभग हर योजना की पारिवारिक पहचान
One family ID and member IDs, used for pensions, ration, Ladli Behna, certificates and scholarships

Find or create
1
You
Check if you already have one पता करें कि पहले से है या नहीं
Most families were registered in surveys. Search on samagra.gov.in by name, mobile or Aadhaar. The family ID is 8 digits and each member ID is 9 digits (as reported).
Tip: Schemes usually ask for the member ID, not the family ID.
2
You
New family or moved to MP? नया परिवार या मप्र में आए हैं?
Apply for a new family ID at your Gram Panchayat, Janpad or urban ward office, or through the new-family option on the portal. Take Aadhaar of all members and address proof.
3
You
Do eKYC for every member हर सदस्य का ई-केवाईसी करें
Samagra eKYC links each member to Aadhaar. Do it on the portal with an OTP, or at an MP Online kiosk / CSC, where it is reported to be free.
Stuck? Name or date of birth differs between Samagra and Aadhaar? Correct it first, or eKYC will fail.
Keep it correct
4
Government
Changes are verified बदलाव सत्यापित होते हैं
Adding a member, splitting a family or correcting details is verified by the Panchayat or ward office.
Stuck? Request pending for weeks? Ask the Panchayat secretary or ward office with the request number.
5
Done
Samagra ready for schemes योजनाओं के लिए समग्र तैयार
With eKYC done, your member ID works for certificates, ration, pensions, Ladli Behna and scholarships.
Stuck on Samagra? Complain.
Write to the Janpad / ward office, or complain on the CM Helpline 181 with your Samagra and request numbers.
Portal: samagra.gov.in · eKYC at MP Online kiosks and CSCs (reported free)
