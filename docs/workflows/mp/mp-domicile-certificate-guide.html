Domicile Certificate (MP): Mool Niwasi Praman Patra

मूल निवासी प्रमाण पत्र (मध्य प्रदेश)
Needed for state-quota admissions, MP government jobs and state scholarships
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Certificate ready
Before you apply
1
You
Check who can get it पात्रता जांचें
People who live permanently in Madhya Pradesh. Read the admission or job notice for any residence-period rule.
Tip: Almost every MP service needs a Samagra member ID with eKYC done. No Samagra yet? See the Samagra ID (MP) guide first.
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागज़ तैयार रखें
  • Samagra member ID
  • Aadhaar
  • Residence proof: ration card, voter ID, electricity bill, school leaving certificate
  • Photo
3
You
Apply online or at a Lok Seva Kendra ऑनलाइन या लोक सेवा केंद्र से आवेदन करें
Apply yourself on mpedistrict.gov.in, or at a Lok Seva Kendra / MP Online kiosk. Keep the application number.
Tip: Applying online yourself is reported to be free or nearly free; kiosks charge a small service fee. Do not pay more.
After you apply
4
Government
Checked by the revenue office राजस्व कार्यालय द्वारा जांच
The revenue office checks your residence documents; a local inquiry may be made.
Stuck? Asked for more documents? Upload them against the same application number instead of applying again.
5
Government
Tehsildar / SDM approves तहसीलदार / एसडीएम स्वीकृति देते हैं
The Tehsildar / SDM approves and the certificate is digitally signed.
6
Done
Download your certificate प्रमाण पत्र डाउनलोड करें
Download it from e-District with your application number and check every detail.
Tip: The domicile certificate is reported to be valid for life. Keep printed copies for admissions.
Taking too long? Use your rights.
Certificates are covered by the MP Lok Seva Guarantee Act, with a fixed time limit for each service. If it is crossed, file a first appeal, or complain to the CM Helpline 181, with your application number.
Apply / status: mpedistrict.gov.in or any Lok Seva Kendra / MP Online kiosk · Complaints: CM Helpline 181
