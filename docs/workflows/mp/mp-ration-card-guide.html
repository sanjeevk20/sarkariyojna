Ration in MP: Get your eligibility slip (patrata parchi)

मध्य प्रदेश में राशन: पात्रता पर्ची कैसे मिले
Monthly food grain: 35 kg per Antyodaya family, 5 kg per person for priority households

Before you apply
1
You
Know how MP ration works मप्र में राशन कैसे मिलता है
In MP, ration is linked to your Samagra family ID. Eligible families get an eligibility slip (patrata parchi); the fair price shop serves you against it.
Tip: Almost every MP service needs a Samagra member ID with eKYC done. No Samagra yet? See the Samagra ID (MP) guide first.
2
You
Check if you are already eligible जांचें कि आप पहले से पात्र हैं या नहीं
On the state food (Ration Mitra) portal, download the current month's eligibility slip with your family ID, member ID or Aadhaar.
Tip: Already listed? You only need the slip, not a new card.
3
You
Apply if not listed सूची में नहीं हैं तो आवेदन करें
Apply through the Gram Panchayat / Janpad or urban body office, with Samagra eKYC done for all members and proof of your eligible category.
After you apply
4
Government
Verification सत्यापन
The local body and food department verify the family and category, and add you to the eligible list.
Stuck? Rejected? Ask for the reason in writing and approach the district food office.
5
Done
Collect ration monthly हर महीने राशन लें
At the fair price shop, a member gives an Aadhaar fingerprint on the POS machine and collects grain for all members.
Stuck? Shop gives less or asks for money? Ask for a receipt and complain on the CM Helpline 181.
Short supply or slip pending? Complain.
Complain to the district food office or the CM Helpline 181 with your Samagra family ID.
