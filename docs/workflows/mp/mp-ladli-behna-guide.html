Ladli Behna Yojana: ₹1,500 a month for women in MP

मुख्यमंत्री लाड़ली बहना योजना: महिलाओं को हर महीने ₹1,500
₹1,500 a month by DBT into the woman's own bank account (most 2026 reports)

Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
  • Woman aged 21 to 60, living in Madhya Pradesh
  • Married, widowed, divorced or abandoned
  • Family income up to about ₹2.5 lakh a year; no income-tax payer or government employee in the family
2
You
Know if registration is open जानें पंजीकरण खुला है या नहीं
New registrations have been closed most of the time since 2023, with occasional windows (for example for women newly turning 21). Ask your Gram Panchayat / ward office or Anganwadi before going anywhere.
Stuck? Anyone offering to 'add your name' for money while registration is closed is cheating you.
3
You
Keep these ready ये तैयार रखें
  • Samagra member ID with eKYC
  • Aadhaar
  • Your own bank account, Aadhaar-linked with DBT on
  • Mobile number
Tip: Almost every MP service needs a Samagra member ID with eKYC done. No Samagra yet? See the Samagra ID (MP) guide first.
After you apply
4
Government
Verification and approval सत्यापन और स्वीकृति
Applications taken at camps are checked against Samagra, income and tax data, then approved.
Stuck? Shown as rejected? Ask the Gram Panchayat / ward office for the reason; if it is wrong, raise it with the CM Helpline 181.
5
You
Keep eKYC and bank link current ई-केवाईसी और बैंक लिंक चालू रखें
Complete the yearly eKYC when the window opens (it ran 1 June – 15 July in 2026, as reported) and keep your bank account active.
Stuck? Missed eKYC is the most common reason payments stop. Do it at an MP Online kiosk or CSC; it is reported to be free.
6
Done
₹1,500 every month हर महीने ₹1,500
Money comes by DBT, usually around the 10th of the month. Check your instalment history on cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in.
Stuck? Not received? Check the portal status, then the bank for Aadhaar seeding and DBT. Still stuck: CM Helpline 181.
Payment missing? Complain.
Use the CM Helpline 181 or the Ladli Behna helpline listed on the portal, with your registration number. You can also write to the district Women and Child Development office.
