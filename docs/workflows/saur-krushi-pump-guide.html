Magel Tyala Saur Krushi Pump: Solar pump for daytime irrigation

मागेल त्याला सौर कृषी पंप: दिवसा सिंचनासाठी सौर पंप
Off-grid solar pump for just 10% of the cost (5% for SC / ST), with a 5-year warranty
You do thisMSEDCL / supplier does this (you wait)Pump running
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
  • Farmer in Maharashtra with land in your name
  • A reliable water source: well, borewell, farm pond, or perennial river / stream (own or shared)
  • No existing farm electricity connection
  • Have not received a solar pump under Atal / Mukhyamantri Saur Krushi Pump or KUSUM before
Tip: No water source yet? See the Magel Tyala Shettale (farm pond) or well schemes first.
2
You
Know your pump size पंपाची क्षमता ओळखा
  • Land up to 2.5 acres → 3 HP
  • 2.51 to 5 acres → 5 HP
  • Above 5 acres → 7.5 HP
You may ask for a smaller pump than your tier.
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • 7/12 showing your land and the water source
  • Aadhaar, bank details
  • Caste certificate, if SC / ST (for the 5% share)
  • Consent of co-owners, if the land or water source is joint
Stuck? Water source not recorded on 7/12, or land not in your name? Fix it at the Talathi office first. See the Ferfar guide.
4
You
Apply on the MSEDCL portal महावितरण पोर्टलवर अर्ज करा
Apply online on Mahavitaran (MSEDCL)'s Magel Tyala Saur Krushi Pump portal (portal.mahadiscom.in/solar_MTSKPY). Note your beneficiary / application ID.
Tip: Applying is free. Beware of fake websites and agents asking for money to "register" you.
After you apply
5
MSEDCL / Supplier
Scrutiny and payment demand छाननी व हिस्सा भरणा सूचना
MSEDCL checks your application and documents, then asks you to pay your share: about 10% of the pump cost (5% for SC / ST).
Stuck? Application rejected? Usually the water source or land record does not match. Correct the 7/12 and reapply.
6
You
Pay your share तुमचा हिस्सा भरा
Pay online through the portal only and keep the receipt. Choose or confirm the empanelled supplier as shown on the portal.
Stuck? Never pay a supplier or agent in cash. Only payments on the official portal count.
7
MSEDCL / Supplier
Survey and installation सर्वेक्षण व बसवणी
The supplier surveys your site and installs the solar panels and pump, usually within about two months of the work order. A joint inspection confirms it works.
Stuck? Installation delayed? Raise a complaint on the MSEDCL portal with your beneficiary ID.
8
Done
Free daytime irrigation दिवसा मोफत सिंचन
Irrigate in the daytime with no electricity bill. The supplier gives a 5-year warranty with repair and maintenance.
Tip: Pump not working? Contact the supplier first (under warranty), then MSEDCL. Keep panels clean and the site secure.
Stuck for long? Complain.
Use the complaint option on the MSEDCL solar pump portal with your beneficiary ID, or write to your MSEDCL sub-division office and take an acknowledgement (पोच).
Apply: portal.mahadiscom.in/solar_MTSKPY · Share and pump sizes as per MSEDCL FAQ; check the current notice
