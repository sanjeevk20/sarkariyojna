PM-KISAN: How to get ₹6,000 a year

पीएम-किसान: वर्षाला ₹6,000 कसे मिळवायचे
₹2,000 straight into your bank account, 3 times a year
You do this Government does this (you wait) Money received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
You can apply if farmland is in your name on the 7/12.
You cannot apply if anyone in your family pays income tax, is a government employee, is a doctor / lawyer / CA / engineer in practice, or gets a pension of ₹10,000+ a month.
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Aadhaar card, with your mobile number linked
  • 7/12 extract showing your name
  • Bank passbook (account must be linked to Aadhaar)
Stuck? Land still in your father's name? Get the ferfar (mutation) done at the Talathi office first.
3
You
Apply अर्ज करा
Choose any one:
  • Website pmkisan.gov.in → "New Farmer Registration"
  • PM-KISAN mobile app
  • Nearest CSC / e-Seva Kendra, or ask your Talathi / Krushi Sahayak
Note down your registration number.
After you apply
4
Government
Your land is checked जमिनीची तपासणी
Revenue officials match your name with the 7/12 record.
Stuck? Name or Gat number does not match → visit the Talathi with your 7/12 and Aadhaar.
5
Government
Your Aadhaar and bank are checked आधार व बँक तपासणी
Your name must be spelled the same on Aadhaar, 7/12 and bank account.
Stuck? Spelling differs → correct it. Bank not linked → go to your bank and ask for "Aadhaar seeding and DBT on".
6
You
Do eKYC ई-केवायसी करा
On pmkisan.gov.in (OTP on your Aadhaar mobile) or face scan in the app. Takes 2 minutes.
Important: Without eKYC, your money stops.
7
Government
State approves you राज्य सरकारची मंजुरी
Your status shows RFT signed by State Govt
This means: Maharashtra has approved you. Just wait.
8
Government
Money is being sent पैसे पाठवले जात आहेत
Your status shows FTO generated, payment confirmation pending
This means: payment order has gone to your bank.
Stuck? Money not received → bank account closed or not linked. Fix at bank (see step 5).
9
Done
₹2,000 in your account पैसे खात्यात जमा
You get an SMS. This repeats every 4 months, as long as your eKYC, bank link and land record stay correct.
Check your status anytime: pmkisan.gov.in → "Know Your Status" (registration number or Aadhaar)
