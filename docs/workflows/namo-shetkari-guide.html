Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi: Extra ₹6,000 a year on top of PM-KISAN

नमो शेतकरी महासन्मान निधी: पीएम-किसानसोबत वर्षाला अतिरिक्त ₹6,000
₹2,000 three times a year from Maharashtra, so ₹12,000 a year with PM-KISAN

Make sure you qualify
1
You
No separate form needed वेगळा अर्ज लागत नाही
If you are an approved PM-KISAN beneficiary in Maharashtra, you are added to Namo Shetkari automatically.
Tip: Anyone asking money to "register" you for Namo Shetkari is cheating you.
2
You
Be active in PM-KISAN पीएम-किसानमध्ये सक्रिय राहा
Your PM-KISAN registration must be approved and active, with land in your name. See the PM-KISAN guide if you are not registered.
Stuck? PM-KISAN stopped? Namo Shetkari stops too. Fix PM-KISAN first.
3
You
Keep eKYC done ई-केवायसी पूर्ण ठेवा
Complete eKYC on pmkisan.gov.in (OTP) or by face authentication in the app.
Stuck? eKYC pending is the most common reason both payments stop.
4
You
Link Aadhaar to your bank आधार बँकेशी लिंक करा
Your bank account must be Aadhaar-seeded with DBT on. Ask your bank to confirm.
5
You
Check land seeding जमीन नोंद (लँड सीडिंग) तपासा
Your 7/12 land details must be verified and linked to your record. Check "land seeding" in your status.
Stuck? Land seeding shows No? Contact the Talathi / Krushi Sahayak with your 7/12. Land still in a parent's name? See the Ferfar guide.
Payment
6
Government
State releases the instalment राज्य हप्ता वितरित करते
The state prepares the list from PM-KISAN data (after eKYC, land and bank checks) and releases each instalment by DBT.
7
Done
₹2,000 in your account ₹2,000 खात्यात जमा
You get an SMS. Together with PM-KISAN you receive ₹12,000 a year.
Stuck? Getting PM-KISAN but not Namo Shetkari? Check your status on the Namo Shetkari portal, then contact the Taluka Agriculture Officer.
Instalment missing? Follow up.
Check status on the official Namo Shetkari (NSMNY) portal and pmkisan.gov.in. Then write to the Taluka Agriculture Officer with your PM-KISAN registration number and take an acknowledgement (पोच).
