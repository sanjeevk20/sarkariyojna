7/12 Ferfar: How to get your name on the land record

७/१२ फेरफार: जमिनीच्या उताऱ्यावर तुमचे नाव कसे लावायचे
Needed after you buy, inherit or get land as a gift
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Name on 7/12
Start
1
You
Know your ferfar type फेरफाराचा प्रकार ओळखा
  • Bought land with a registered sale deed → ferfar starts automatically. Go to step 3.
  • Inherited (वारस), gift or family partition → you must apply. Go to step 2.
Tip: Buying land? The ferfar is linked to your registration. You do not need to pay anyone to "start" it.
2
You
Apply with papers (inheritance / gift) कागदपत्रांसह अर्ज करा
  • Death certificate (for inheritance)
  • List of all legal heirs with their Aadhaar
  • Current 7/12 and 8A extract
  • Self-declaration / affidavit about the heirs
Apply at the Talathi office, or online where the state's e-Hakk service is available.
Stuck? Every heir's name must be included. A missing heir can object later and stop the ferfar.
Government process
3
Government
Ferfar entry is created फेरफार नोंद तयार होते
The Talathi creates a ferfar entry with a number. For a sale, the details come automatically from the Sub-Registrar's office.
Note down your ferfar number.
Stuck? No entry after a few weeks? Visit the Talathi with your registered document number.
4
Government
Notice to everyone concerned संबंधितांना नोटीस
A notice goes to the seller / all heirs / bank. They get 15 days to raise an objection (हरकत).
Stuck? Someone objected? It becomes a dispute case. The Circle Officer hears both sides. Keep your papers ready. You can appeal the decision to the SDO.
5
Government
Circle Officer approves मंडळ अधिकाऱ्यांची मंजुरी
After the notice period, if there is no objection, the Circle Officer (मंडळ अधिकारी) certifies the ferfar.
Stuck? Rejected? Read the reason, fix the papers and apply again, or appeal.
6
Done
Your name is on the 7/12 ७/१२ वर तुमचे नाव
The 7/12 now shows your name with the ferfar number. Download the digitally signed 7/12 from digitalsatbara.mahabhumi.gov.in. It is valid without a Talathi stamp.
Notice period over? No reason to wait.
If 15 days have passed with no objection and the ferfar is still not certified, give a written application to the Tahsildar with your ferfar number and take an acknowledgement (पोच).
Check notice and status: Aapli Chawadi / pending ferfar on bhulekh.mahabhumi.gov.in (ferfar number, objection last date)
Never pay to "speed up", the 15-day notice is the law · Use only .gov.in websites
