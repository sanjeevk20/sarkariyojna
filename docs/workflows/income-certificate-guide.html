Income Certificate: How to get it on Aaple Sarkar

उत्पन्नाचा दाखला: आपले सरकार वरून कसा मिळवायचा
Needed for scholarships, fee concession, EWS and many schemes
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Certificate ready
Before you apply
1
You
Check which certificate you need कोणता दाखला हवा ते तपासा
Ask the scheme or office: which year's income (1 year or 3 years) and in whose name (yours or your parent's). Most schemes want a certificate from the current financial year.
Stuck? Have last year's certificate? Most schemes reject it. Apply fresh, early.
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Identity: Aadhaar / PAN / Voter ID
  • Address: Ration card / Electricity bill
  • Income: Job → Form 16 or salary slip · Farmer → 7/12 and 8A · Others → self-declaration
  • Passport-size photo
Tip: No stamp-paper affidavit needed. A signed self-declaration is enough.
3
You
Apply अर्ज करा
Choose any one:
  • Website aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in → Revenue Department → Income Certificate
  • Nearest Aaple Sarkar Seva Kendra / Maha e-Seva Kendra
Pay the small fee. Note down your Application ID and the time limit (days) shown.
Tip: At a Seva Kendra pay only the fixed fee and take a receipt. Never pay an agent extra.
After you apply
4
Government
Your papers are checked कागदपत्रांची तपासणी
The Tahsil office checks your form and documents.
Stuck? Status says sent back? Read the remark, upload the missing paper and resubmit online. No visit needed.
5
Government
Income is verified (if needed) उत्पन्नाची पडताळणी
For farm or business income, the Talathi may be asked for a report.
Stuck? Pending at Talathi for long? Visit the Talathi office with your Application ID.
6
Government
Tahsildar approves तहसीलदारांची मंजुरी
Tahsildar / Naib Tahsildar approves and signs digitally.
7
Done
Download your certificate दाखला डाउनलोड करा
Log in to Aaple Sarkar and download it. It is digitally signed, so it is valid without any stamp.
Late? It's your right.
If the time limit has passed, you can file a First Appeal on Aaple Sarkar with your Application ID, under the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015.
Check status anytime: Aaple Sarkar → Track your application (Application ID)
