Caste Certificate: How to get it on Aaple Sarkar

जातीचा दाखला: आपले सरकार वरून कसा मिळवायचा
Needed for reservation in education, jobs, scholarships and elections

Before you apply
1
You
Know which certificate you need कोणते प्रमाणपत्र हवे ते ओळखा
  • Caste certificate: issued by the SDO. This guide.
  • Non-Creamy Layer: also needed for OBC / VJNT / SBC benefits, renewed regularly.
  • Caste validity: needed for professional courses, many jobs and elections. Apply after you get the caste certificate.
2
You
Find an old proof of caste जुना जातीचा पुरावा शोधा
This is the most important step. You need a document of you, your father or grandfather (father's side) showing the caste and living in Maharashtra before:
  • SC: 10 Aug 1950 · ST: 6 Sep 1950
  • VJ / NT: 21 Nov 1961 · OBC / SBC: 13 Oct 1967
Examples: father's or grandfather's school leaving certificate, birth / death register extract, old land record.
Stuck? No old document at home? Ask relatives: an uncle's (father's brother's) school record also works. Check the old village school register and Gram Panchayat birth register.
3
You
Keep other papers ready इतर कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Aadhaar and address proof
  • Your school leaving certificate showing caste
  • Family tree (वंशावळ) showing how you are related to the person in the old document
  • Self-declaration / affidavit, photo
Tip: Documents from your mother's side usually do not count. Caste follows the father's side.
4
You
Apply अर्ज करा
Choose any one:
  • aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in → Revenue Department → Caste Certificate
  • Nearest Aaple Sarkar Seva Kendra, or a special camp at your school / college
Note your Application ID and the time limit (days) shown.
Tip: Pay only the fixed fee and take a receipt. Never pay an agent to "arrange" old documents.
After you apply
5
Government
Your papers are checked कागदपत्रांची तपासणी
The Tahsil / SDO office checks your old proof and family tree. A local inquiry may be done.
Stuck? Status says sent back? Usually the old proof or family tree is unclear. Upload a clearer copy or an extra document and resubmit.
6
Government
SDO approves उपविभागीय अधिकाऱ्यांची मंजुरी
The Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO), or the competent officer in your taluka, approves and signs digitally.
7
Done
Download your certificate दाखला डाउनलोड करा
Log in to Aaple Sarkar and download it. It is digitally signed and does not expire. Keep it safe.
Tip: Your certificate later helps your brothers, sisters and children prove their caste.
Late? It's your right.
If the time limit has passed, you can file a First Appeal on Aaple Sarkar with your Application ID, under the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015.
Next: apply for Caste Validity if your course or job needs it, early, as it takes time
