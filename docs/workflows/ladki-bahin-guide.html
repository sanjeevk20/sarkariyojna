Ladki Bahin Yojana: How to get ₹1,500 a month

मुख्यमंत्री माझी लाडकी बहीण योजना: दरमहा ₹1,500 कसे मिळवायचे
₹1,500 every month straight into your bank account
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Money received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
  • Woman aged 21 to 65, living in Maharashtra
  • Married, widowed, divorced, abandoned or destitute, or one unmarried woman per family
  • Family income up to ₹2.5 lakh a year
2
You
Check who cannot apply कोण अर्ज करू शकत नाही
You cannot apply if anyone in your family:
  • Pays income tax, or is a government employee / pensioner
  • Owns a four-wheeler (tractor is allowed)
  • Is an MP / MLA or holds a board / corporation post
Or if you already get ₹1,500 or more from another government scheme.
Stuck? Applying when not eligible can mean the money is stopped and recovered later. Be honest.
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Aadhaar card
  • Domicile proof: domicile certificate, or old ration card / voter ID / school leaving certificate / birth certificate
  • Income certificate
  • Ration card, photo
  • Bank account linked to Aadhaar
  • Signed undertaking (हमीपत्र)
Tip: Have a yellow or orange ration card? You usually don't need an income certificate. Born outside Maharashtra but married here? Your husband's domicile documents can be used.
4
You
Apply (it is free) अर्ज करा (मोफत)
Choose any one:
  • Website ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in
  • Your Anganwadi Sevika, or the ward / Gram Panchayat help desk
Note your application number.
Tip: Applying is free. Never pay anyone to fill the form.
After you apply
5
Government
Your application is checked अर्जाची तपासणी
Women and Child Development officials check your form and documents.
Stuck? Rejected or sent back? Read the reason on the portal or ask your Anganwadi Sevika. Correct it and resubmit.
6
Government
Approved: bank and eKYC मंजुरी: बँक व ई-केवायसी
Your name goes on the approved list. Your bank account must be Aadhaar-seeded with DBT on. Complete eKYC on the portal when asked.
Stuck? eKYC not done? Your money can stop. Do it on the portal with the OTP on your Aadhaar mobile.
7
Done
₹1,500 in your account every month दरमहा ₹1,500 खात्यात जमा
The money comes by DBT. You get an SMS from your bank.
Stuck? Approved but no money? Your bank account may not be Aadhaar-seeded or may be inactive. Ask the bank for "Aadhaar seeding and DBT on".
Money not coming? Complain.
Raise a grievance on the Ladki Bahin portal with your application number, or ask your Anganwadi Sevika / the district Women and Child Development office.
Keep your eKYC, bank link and details correct, the government may re-verify beneficiaries
