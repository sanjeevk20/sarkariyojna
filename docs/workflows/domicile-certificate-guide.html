Domicile Certificate: How to get it on Aaple Sarkar

अधिवास प्रमाणपत्र: आपले सरकार वरून कसे मिळवायचे
Needed for state-quota college admission, state jobs and scholarships

Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
The official name is Age, Nationality and Domicile Certificate. You can apply if you have been living in Maharashtra for at least 10 years. For a child, the parent's residence can be used.
Tip: Admission seasons (CET, NEET, JEE) bring a rush. Apply before admission forms open.
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Age proof: birth certificate or school leaving certificate
  • Identity: Aadhaar / PAN / Voter ID
  • 10 years of residence: any mix of ration card, electricity bills, property tax receipts, school bonafide certificates, 7/12
  • Self-declaration, photo
Stuck? Missing some years? Combine documents: school bonafide certificates for each school, old electricity bills, rent agreement or property tax receipts together can cover 10 years.
3
You
Apply अर्ज करा
Choose any one:
  • aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in → Revenue Department → Age, Nationality and Domicile Certificate
  • Nearest Aaple Sarkar Seva Kendra / Maha e-Seva Kendra
Pay the small fee. Note your Application ID and the time limit (days) shown.
Tip: Pay only the fixed fee and take a receipt. Never pay an agent extra.
After you apply
4
Government
Your papers are checked कागदपत्रांची तपासणी
The Tahsil office checks your age proof and that your residence documents cover 10 years.
Stuck? Status says sent back? Usually a gap in residence years or an unclear scan. Upload the missing year's proof and resubmit. No visit needed.
5
Government
Tahsildar approves तहसीलदारांची मंजुरी
The Tahsildar approves and signs digitally.
6
Done
Download your certificate प्रमाणपत्र डाउनलोड करा
Log in to Aaple Sarkar and download it. It is digitally signed, valid without any stamp, and usually does not expire.
Tip: Keep a few printouts and a PDF on your phone. Colleges ask for it at every admission round.
Late? It's your right.
If the time limit has passed, you can file a First Appeal on Aaple Sarkar with your Application ID, under the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015.
Check status anytime: Aaple Sarkar → Track your application (Application ID)
