PM Ujjwala Yojana: How to get a free LPG connection

प्रधानमंत्री उज्ज्वला योजना: मोफत गॅस जोडणी कशी मिळवायची
Free LPG connection for women of poor households, plus subsidy on refills
You do thisGas agency does this (you wait)Cooking on LPG
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
  • A woman aged 18 or more
  • No LPG connection in anyone's name in the household
  • From a poor household, e.g. SC / ST, Antyodaya (AAY) ration card, PMAY-Gramin beneficiary, forest dweller, or poor family by self-declaration
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Your Aadhaar
  • Ration card showing family members, and Aadhaar of the adult members on it
  • Bank account (passbook with IFSC), linked to Aadhaar
  • Photo
Tip: Migrant family without a local ration card? A self-declaration of address is usually accepted.
3
You
Apply (it is free) अर्ज करा (मोफत)
Choose any one:
  • Website pmuy.gov.in → choose company (Indane / Bharat Gas / HP Gas) → choose nearest distributor
  • Go directly to your nearest gas distributor with papers
Note your application number.
Tip: The connection is free. Never pay an agent or the distributor extra for "quick" approval.
After you apply
4
Gas agency
Papers and family are checked कागदपत्रे व कुटुंबाची तपासणी
The distributor completes your KYC and checks that no one in your family already has an LPG connection.
Stuck? Rejected for "existing connection"? Someone on your ration card may already have one. Surrender the old connection or correct the ration card, then reapply.
5
Gas agency
Connection is installed गॅस जोडणी बसवली जाते
You get the cylinder, regulator, pipe and booklet without deposit. The distributor installs it and shows you safety steps.
Tip: Ask for the safety demonstration. Check the pipe and regulator are fitted properly.
6
You
Complete eKYC and bank link ई-केवायसी व बँक लिंक करा
Do Aadhaar eKYC at the distributor or on the gas company's app when asked. Make sure your bank account is Aadhaar-seeded so the subsidy reaches you.
Stuck? Subsidy not coming? Usually eKYC is pending or the bank isn't Aadhaar-seeded. Fix both.
7
Done
Cook on LPG, get refill subsidy गॅसवर स्वयंपाक, रिफिलवर अनुदान
Book refills by phone / app. A subsidy of about ₹300 per cylinder comes into your bank account (up to a set number of refills each year). Under Ujjwala 2.0 the first refill and stove are free.
Tip: Ask your distributor about any Maharashtra free-refill scheme you may also be eligible for.
Problem with the agency? Complain.
First complain to the distributor in writing. If not solved, use the gas company's customer grievance portal or CPGRAMS. Gas leak emergency: call 1906.
Check status: pmuy.gov.in or your gas company's website / app (application number)
