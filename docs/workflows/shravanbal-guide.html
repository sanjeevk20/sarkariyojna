Shravanbal Yojana: Monthly pension for senior citizens

श्रावणबाळ सेवा राज्य निवृत्तिवेतन योजना: ज्येष्ठ नागरिकांना दरमहा निवृत्तिवेतन
Monthly pension (about ₹1,500) for elderly people aged 65+, paid into the bank
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Pension received
Before you apply
1
You / family
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
  • Aged 65 or more
  • Living in Maharashtra for many years
  • Very low family income and no regular support
There are two groups: families on the BPL list (state + central pension together) and families not on the BPL list (state pension).
Tip: Below 65 and a widow, disabled or seriously ill? See Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Yojana instead.
2
You / family
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Age proof: school leaving certificate, birth certificate, voter ID, or age certificate from a government doctor
  • Residence proof
  • Income certificate from the Tahsildar
  • BPL card / list extract, if on the BPL list
  • Aadhaar, photo, bank passbook (account linked to Aadhaar)
Stuck? No birth or school record? A government doctor's age certificate is usually accepted.
3
You / family
Apply (it is free) अर्ज करा (मोफत)
Choose any one:
  • Aaple Sarkar portal / Seva Kendra
  • Sanjay Gandhi Yojana branch at your Tahsil office, or ask your Talathi for help
A family member can apply on behalf of the elderly person. Note the application number.
Tip: Applying is free. Never pay an agent to "get it sanctioned".
After you apply
4
Government
Papers are checked कागदपत्रांची तपासणी
The Tahsil office checks age, residence and income. The Talathi may do a local inquiry.
Stuck? Asked for more papers? Submit them quickly with the application number and take an acknowledgement.
5
Government
Taluka committee approves तालुका समितीची मंजुरी
The taluka-level Sanjay Gandhi Yojana committee also approves Shravanbal applications at its regular meetings.
Stuck? Rejected? Ask for the reason in writing. Fix the missing proof and apply again.
6
Done
Pension every month दरमहा निवृत्तिवेतन जमा
The pension comes by DBT into the Aadhaar-linked bank account every month.
Stuck? Approved but no money? Check the bank account is Aadhaar-seeded with DBT on and active.
7
You / family
Keep it running निवृत्तिवेतन चालू ठेवा
  • Submit the life certificate (हयातीचा दाखला) every year when asked
  • Keep the bank account active
  • Tell the office about any change of address or bank
Stuck? Pension suddenly stopped? Usually the life certificate was not submitted. Family members: set a yearly reminder.
Pending too long? Follow up.
Give a written application to the Tahsildar / Naib Tahsildar (Sanjay Gandhi Yojana) with the application number and take an acknowledgement (पोच). You can also raise a grievance on Aaple Sarkar.
Check status: Aaple Sarkar (application number) or Tahsil office
