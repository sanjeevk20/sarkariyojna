Caste Validity Certificate: How to get it

जात वैधता प्रमाणपत्र: कसे मिळवायचे
Needed for reserved seats in professional courses, reserved government jobs and elections
You do thisCommittee does this (you wait)Validity ready
Before you apply
1
You
Know when you need it कधी लागते ते ओळखा
  • Admission on a reserved seat in engineering, medical, pharmacy and other professional courses
  • Government job on a reserved post
  • Election from a reserved seat
Tip: Students: apply in 11th or 12th, not after the CET result. It can take months.
2
You
Have your caste certificate first आधी जातीचा दाखला हवा
The committee checks whether your caste certificate is genuine. You must already have it.
Stuck? No caste certificate yet? See the Caste Certificate guide first.
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Your caste certificate and school leaving certificate
  • The old proof of caste before the cut-off date (same as for the caste certificate)
  • Family tree (वंशावळ) and affidavit in the prescribed format
  • College bonafide / form (for students)
Tip: Does your father, brother, sister or uncle (father's side) already have a caste validity? Attach it. It is the strongest proof and makes the process much faster.
4
You
Apply and submit अर्ज करा आणि सादर करा
Fill the form online on the state's caste validity portal (CCVIS), print it, and submit it with your documents to the District Caste Certificate Scrutiny Committee (students often submit through their college).
Keep the acknowledgement / token number safe.
Tip: For admissions, colleges usually accept the acknowledgement of your validity application for provisional admission. Ask your college.
After you apply
5
Scrutiny Committee
Committee checks your case समितीकडून तपासणी
The committee examines your documents. If the old proof is weak, it may send a vigilance team (दक्षता पथक) to inquire locally, or call you for a hearing.
Stuck? Got a hearing notice? Attend on the date with all original documents. Missing a hearing delays your case.
6
Scrutiny Committee
Committee decides समितीचा निर्णय
If satisfied, the committee issues the caste validity certificate.
Stuck? Caste claim invalidated? This decision can be challenged in the High Court. Get legal advice quickly.
7
Done
Collect your validity certificate वैधता प्रमाणपत्र मिळवा
Download or collect it. It is valid for life.
Tip: Your validity becomes the strongest proof for your brothers, sisters and children when they apply.
Pending too long? Escalate.
Give a written reminder to the Member Secretary of the District Caste Certificate Scrutiny Committee with your token number and take an acknowledgement (पोच). If an admission or job deadline is near, mention it in writing.
Start early, this is the slowest certificate in the chain: caste certificate → caste validity → admission / job
Use only official government websites · Never pay anyone to "manage" the committee
