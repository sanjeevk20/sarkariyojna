Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Jan Arogya Yojana: Free hospital treatment

महात्मा ज्योतिराव फुले जन आरोग्य योजना: मोफत रुग्णालय उपचार
Cashless treatment up to about ₹5 lakh per family per year at network hospitals
You do thisHospital / scheme does thisTreated, no bill
Before you need treatment
1
You / family
Check you are covered तुम्ही पात्र आहात का ते तपासा
Families living in Maharashtra with a ration card (any colour) or domicile proof are covered. Many surgeries and treatments (heart, cancer, kidney, accidents, etc.) are included. The scheme works together with Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY).
Tip: Road accident in Maharashtra? Treatment may be covered even if you are not a resident. Ask at the hospital.
2
You / family
Get your health card आरोग्य कार्ड मिळवा
Make your Ayushman / MJPJAY e-card (free) using Aadhaar:
  • On the Ayushman app with Aadhaar OTP / face authentication
  • At a CSC / e-Seva Kendra or the hospital's help desk
Keep Aadhaar and ration card ready.
Tip: Make cards for every family member now, not during an emergency.
3
You / family
Go to a network hospital नेटवर्क रुग्णालयात जा
Only empanelled (network) hospitals give cashless treatment. Find them on jeevandayee.gov.in or the app. At the hospital, go to the Aarogyamitra (आरोग्यमित्र) desk.
Stuck? Hospital is not in the network? Ask the Aarogyamitra or helpline for the nearest network hospital for your treatment.
At the hospital
4
Hospital / Scheme
Hospital asks for approval रुग्णालय मंजुरी मागते
The doctor diagnoses you. The Aarogyamitra sends a pre-authorization request to the scheme with your reports.
In an emergency, treatment should start immediately; approval is sought alongside.
Stuck? Approval delayed? Ask the Aarogyamitra for the status. Missing reports are the usual reason.
5
Hospital / Scheme
Cashless treatment कॅशलेस उपचार
Your treatment, medicines, tests, stay and food for the covered procedure are paid by the scheme. You should not pay for them.
Stuck? Asked to pay a deposit or for covered items? Refuse politely, ask for it in writing, and call the scheme helpline.
6
Done
Discharge and follow-up डिस्चार्ज व पाठपुरावा
Sign the discharge papers and keep a copy of all reports. Follow-up visits and medicines for the covered procedure are also included for a period.
Tip: Keep the discharge summary safe. You will need it for future treatment.
Asked to pay? Cashless treatment is your right.
Call the MJPJAY / Ayushman helpline or contact the district coordinator of the scheme. Note the hospital name, date and amount demanded. You can also file a written complaint with the hospital and the district office.
Find hospitals and procedures: jeevandayee.gov.in · Card: Ayushman app / beneficiary.nha.gov.in
