PM Awas Yojana (Gramin): How to get a pucca house

प्रधानमंत्री आवास योजना (ग्रामीण): पक्के घर कसे मिळवायचे
About ₹1.2 lakh in instalments to build your house, plus MGNREGA wages and toilet support
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)House complete
Getting selected
1
You
Know which scheme is yours तुमची योजना कोणती ते ओळखा
  • Live in a village → PMAY-Gramin. This guide.
  • Live in a city / town → PMAY-Urban 2.0. Different process.
2
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
You can get it if your family has no pucca house (you live in a kutcha or damaged house, or are homeless).
You usually cannot if the family has a government employee, an income-tax payer, a three / four-wheeler, or already owns a pucca house.
3
You
Get your name on the list यादीत नाव लावा
Selection is from the Awaas+ survey. Ask your Gram Sevak / Gram Panchayat whether you are on the list, or whether a new survey or self-survey (Awaas+ app) is open.
Stuck? Not on the list? Ask the Gram Sevak to include you in the survey. Also ask about the state gharkul schemes (e.g. Ramai, Shabari, Modi Awas) for your category.
4
Government
Gram Sabha checks the list ग्रामसभेत यादी तपासणी
The Gram Sabha verifies the waiting list and its priority order.
Tip: Attend the Gram Sabha. This is where names are confirmed or objected to.
Building your house
5
Government
Your house is sanctioned घरकुल मंजूर
The Panchayat Samiti / BDO sanctions your house. Keep ready: Aadhaar, Aadhaar-linked bank account, MGNREGA job card, and land proof (7/12 or Namuna 8).
Stuck? Land not in your name? Get the ferfar done first. Landless? Ask about the state's land-purchase help for gharkul beneficiaries.
6
You
Build in stages, with photos टप्प्याटप्प्याने बांधकाम, फोटोसह
The first instalment comes after sanction. Build in stages: foundation → walls → roof → finish. At each stage, a geo-tagged photo of your house is uploaded (by the Gram Rozgar Sevak / technical assistant or on the AwaasApp).
Stuck? Next instalment not coming? Usually the stage photo was not uploaded or not inspected. Ask the Gram Rozgar Sevak / Panchayat Samiti engineer.
7
Government
Instalments are paid हप्ते जमा होतात
Each instalment comes by DBT after the stage is verified. You can also get MGNREGA wages for working on your own house and toilet support under Swachh Bharat.
Stuck? Instalment approved but not received? Check that your bank account is Aadhaar-seeded with DBT on.
8
Done
House complete घर पूर्ण
The final geo-tagged photo is uploaded and the house is marked complete. Try to finish within about a year.
Stuck for long? Complain.
Give a written complaint to the BDO, Panchayat Samiti with your PMAY-G registration number and take an acknowledgement (पोच). You can also use the central grievance portal CPGRAMS.
Check status: pmayg.nic.in → beneficiary search (registration number)
Selection and instalments are free, never pay anyone to "get your name" or "release" money · Use only .gov.in / .nic.in websites
