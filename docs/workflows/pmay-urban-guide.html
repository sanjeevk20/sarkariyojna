PM Awas Yojana (Urban 2.0): How to get help for a home in the city

प्रधानमंत्री आवास योजना (शहरी 2.0): शहरात घरासाठी मदत कशी मिळवायची
Help to build, buy or rent a pucca home in cities and towns
You do thisGovernment / bank does this (you wait)Home ready
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
  • You live in a city or town (villages: see PMAY-Gramin)
  • No one in your family owns a pucca house anywhere in India
  • Family income: EWS up to ₹3 lakh · LIG ₹3 to 6 lakh · MIG ₹6 to 9 lakh a year
Tip: The house is usually in the name of the woman of the family, or jointly.
2
You
Pick the option that fits you तुमच्यासाठी योग्य पर्याय निवडा
  • Own land, want to build → BLC: help to build on your own plot (EWS)
  • Want to buy a flat in a government / partner project → AHP (EWS)
  • Taking a home loan from a bank → ISS: interest subsidy (EWS / LIG / MIG)
  • Need to rent (workers, migrants) → ARH: affordable rental
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Aadhaar of all family members
  • Income certificate / ITR
  • Bank account linked to Aadhaar
  • Self-declaration that you own no pucca house
  • For BLC: land / property card in your name
  • Caste certificate, if applicable
Stuck? Plot not in your name? Get the property card / mutation updated first. BLC needs land in the family's name.
4
You
Apply (it is free) अर्ज करा (मोफत)
Choose any one:
  • Website pmay-urban.gov.in (Aadhaar OTP)
  • Your municipal corporation / council PMAY cell
  • For ISS: also through your bank / housing finance company
Note your application number.
Tip: Beware of fake PMAY websites and agents. Applying is free. Never pay to "get selected".
After you apply
5
Government / Bank
Your application is checked अर्जाची तपासणी
The Urban Local Body (municipal corporation / council) verifies your details. For BLC, they also check your plot.
Stuck? Rejected? Common reasons: a family member already owns a pucca house, or income doesn't match. Read the reason, correct it and reapply.
6
Government / Bank
Approval and money मंजुरी आणि रक्कम
  • BLC: money comes in instalments as you build, with geo-tagged photos at each stage
  • AHP: flat allotted (often by lottery)
  • ISS: subsidy credited to your home loan account in yearly instalments, reducing your EMI
Stuck? BLC instalment stuck? Usually the stage photo was not uploaded or inspected. Ask the municipal PMAY cell.
7
Done
Your home is ready तुमचे घर तयार
Your house is built, or you get possession of your flat, or your loan becomes cheaper with the subsidy.
Stuck for long? Complain.
Give a written complaint to your municipal PMAY cell with your application number and take an acknowledgement (पोच). For ISS, complain to your bank first. You can also use CPGRAMS.
Check status: pmay-urban.gov.in → track application (application number)
Use only .gov.in websites · Never pay anyone to "get selected"
