Birsa Munda Krushi Kranti Yojana: Grants for wells and irrigation for tribal farmers

बिरसा मुंडा कृषी क्रांती योजना: आदिवासी शेतकऱ्यांना विहीर व सिंचनासाठी अनुदान
Up to ₹4 lakh for a new well, plus grants for pumps, drip / sprinkler and farm-pond lining
You do thisAgriculture office does this (you wait)Grant received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
  • Farmer from a Scheduled Tribe family in Maharashtra, inside or outside tribal areas
  • Farmland in your name: 0.20 to 6 hectares (at least 0.40 ha for a new well)
  • Forest-rights (वनपट्टा) holders can also apply
  • The old ₹1.5 lakh income limit has been removed under the 2024 GR
Tip: Land below 0.40 ha? Neighbouring tribal farmers can apply jointly for a new well. BPL families are not limited by the 6 ha cap. SC farmers: see the Krushi Swavalamban guide.
2
You
Choose what you need काय हवे ते निवडा
  • New well: up to ₹4 lakh
  • Old well repair: up to ₹1 lakh
  • Borewell (forest-rights holders only): up to ₹50,000
  • In-well boring, pump set, electric connection
  • Farm-pond plastic lining
  • Drip / sprinkler irrigation
Tip: The 12-metre depth condition for new wells has been removed. Check the current notice for each component's limit.
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Scheduled Tribe caste certificate
  • 7/12 and 8A in your name, or forest-rights title (वनपट्टा)
  • Aadhaar, Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Self-declaration / affidavit
  • For a new well: Talathi certificate, groundwater (GSDA) certificate, Gram Sabha resolution
Stuck? Land still in a parent's name? Get the ferfar done first. See the Ferfar guide.
4
You
Apply on MahaDBT महाडीबीटीवर अर्ज करा
Log in on mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in as a Farmer (Aadhaar-based), choose Birsa Munda Krushi Kranti Yojana and the component, upload documents and submit. Note your application ID.
Tip: Selection is by lottery, with priority for BPL and primitive tribal group families. Applying is free.
After you apply
5
Agriculture office
Selection and pre-approval निवड व पूर्वसंमती
If selected in the lottery, the Panchayat Samiti agriculture office checks your documents and gives pre-approval (पूर्वसंमती).
Stuck? Do not start work before pre-approval. Work done earlier is usually not paid.
6
You
Do the work, keep proof काम करा, पुरावे ठेवा
Complete the work within the time given. Keep bills and photos of each stage (geo-tagged where asked) and upload them on MahaDBT.
7
Agriculture office
Inspection तपासणी
The Agriculture Officer inspects and measures the completed work.
Stuck? Inspection delayed? Ask the Panchayat Samiti agriculture office in writing with your application ID.
8
Done
Grant paid to your bank अनुदान बँक खात्यात
The grant is paid by DBT into your Aadhaar-linked bank account. For a new well it may come in stages.
Stuck for long? Escalate.
Write to the Agriculture Officer, Panchayat Samiti, then the Agriculture Development Officer, Zilla Parishad, with your MahaDBT application ID, and take an acknowledgement (पोच). You can also raise a grievance on MahaDBT.
Apply: mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in → Farmer login · Amounts as per the 1 Oct 2024 GR; check the current notice
