Social security pension in Rajasthan

राजस्थान सामाजिक सुरक्षा पेंशन
Monthly pension: ₹1,300 from January 2026 by order; a rise to ₹1,450 was announced in the 2026-27 budget (reported)

Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
  • Old age: women 55+, men 58+
  • Widowed, divorced or abandoned women
  • People with 40% or more disability
  • Family income from all sources up to about ₹48,000 a year, or an eligible category such as BPL (as reported)
2
You
Keep these ready ये तैयार रखें
  • Jan Aadhaar with your name matching Aadhaar
  • Age proof; husband's death certificate for widows; disability certificate
  • Bank account linked to Jan Aadhaar
Tip: Almost every Rajasthan service now starts from your family's Jan Aadhaar. Names must match Aadhaar. No Jan Aadhaar yet? See the Jan Aadhaar guide first.
3
You
Apply at e-Mitra ई-मित्र से आवेदन करें
Apply through e-Mitra or your SSO login. The application goes to the BDO (rural) or SDM / municipal office (urban). Keep the PPO / application number.
After you apply
4
Government
Sanction स्वीकृति
The office checks your details against Jan Aadhaar and sanctions the pension.
Stuck? Rejected? Ask for the reason on ssp.rajasthan.gov.in. A name or age mismatch with Jan Aadhaar is the usual cause; fix Jan Aadhaar and apply again.
5
You
Do the yearly verification सालाना सत्यापन कराएं
Every pensioner must do annual physical verification: at an e-Mitra by fingerprint, or by face on the Rajasthan Social Pension and Aadhaar Face app. The 2026 deadline was 31 March.
Stuck? Missed verification is the most common reason pensions stop. Do it as soon as possible; the stopped amount is usually restored once verified.
6
Done
Pension every month हर महीने पेंशन
The pension comes by DBT. Check payments on ssp.rajasthan.gov.in (Reports) with your Jan Aadhaar or PPO number.
Stuck? Shown as 'dead' or 'out of state' by mistake? Go to the BDO / SDM office with Aadhaar and Jan Aadhaar to be re-verified.
Pension stopped? Complain.
Complain on Rajasthan Sampark 181 with your PPO or Jan Aadhaar number, or write to the BDO / SDM who sanctioned it.
Status: ssp.rajasthan.gov.in · Verification: e-Mitra or the face app · Complaints: Sampark 181
