Caste certificate in Rajasthan (SC / ST / OBC / MBC)

जाति प्रमाण पत्र, राजस्थान
Needed for reservation in jobs and education, scholarships and many schemes

Before you apply
1
You
Check who can get it पात्रता जांचें
Members of a caste on the Rajasthan SC, ST, OBC or MBC list. OBC / MBC non-creamy-layer certificates also need an income check.
Tip: Almost every Rajasthan service now starts from your family's Jan Aadhaar. Names must match Aadhaar. No Jan Aadhaar yet? See the Jan Aadhaar guide first.
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागज़ तैयार रखें
  • Jan Aadhaar and Aadhaar
  • Proof of the family's caste: father's or a relative's caste certificate, or old land / school records showing caste
  • Mool Niwas proof
  • Self-declaration
3
You
Apply online or at e-Mitra ऑनलाइन या ई-मित्र से आवेदन करें
Log in to sso.rajasthan.gov.in, open e-Mitra (citizen login) and apply, or go to any e-Mitra kiosk. Keep the application number.
Tip: From your own citizen login you pay only the government fee; kiosks add a small service charge. Ask for the printed rate list and do not pay more.
After you apply
4
Government
Checked by the revenue office राजस्व कार्यालय द्वारा जांच
The Patwari / revenue office checks the family's caste records. Guides report about 7–10 working days when papers are complete.
Stuck? Asked for more documents? Upload them against the same application number instead of applying again.
5
Government
Tehsildar / SDM approves तहसीलदार / एसडीएम स्वीकृति देते हैं
The Tehsildar / SDM approves and the certificate is digitally signed.
6
Done
Download your certificate प्रमाण पत्र डाउनलोड करें
Download it from SSO / e-Mitra with your application number and check every detail.
Tip: A father's caste certificate makes later certificates for the children much faster. Keep a copy safe.
Taking too long? Use your rights.
Certificates are notified services under the Rajasthan Guaranteed Delivery of Public Services Act, 2011. If the time limit shown when you applied is crossed, or you are refused without a written reason, file a first appeal within 30 days, or complain on Rajasthan Sampark 181 with your application number.
Apply / status: sso.rajasthan.gov.in (citizen login) or any e-Mitra kiosk · Complaints: Rajasthan Sampark 181
