₹9,000 a year for Rajasthan farmers: PM-KISAN + CM Kisan Samman Nidhi

पीएम-किसान + मुख्यमंत्री किसान सम्मान निधि
₹6,000 a year from PM-KISAN plus ₹3,000 a year from the state, by DBT (state says it will rise step by step to ₹12,000 total)
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Money received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
Farmer family with cultivable land in your name in the jamabandi. Income-tax payers, government employees and some professionals are excluded under PM-KISAN.
Stuck? Land still in a parent's name? Do namantaran first; see the Land mutation guide.
2
You
Register for PM-KISAN पीएम-किसान में पंजीकरण
Register on pmkisan.gov.in (New Farmer Registration) or at e-Mitra / CSC with Aadhaar, jamabandi details and bank account.
Tip: Almost every Rajasthan service now starts from your family's Jan Aadhaar. Names must match Aadhaar. No Jan Aadhaar yet? See the Jan Aadhaar guide first.
3
You
Finish eKYC and land seeding ई-केवाईसी और भूमि सीडिंग पूरी करें
Do PM-KISAN eKYC (OTP, fingerprint or face) and make sure land seeding shows Yes and the bank is Aadhaar-seeded.
Stuck? Instalment stopped? Check eKYC, land seeding and Aadhaar-bank seeding on the PM-KISAN status page.
After you apply
4
Government
State and centre approve राज्य और केंद्र स्वीकृति
The state verifies your land record and approves; the centre releases PM-KISAN. The state's ₹3,000 goes to the same PM-KISAN farmers.
5
Government
State instalments राज्य की किस्तें
The state pays its share in separate DBT instalments (for example about ₹718 crore to 72 lakh farmers in October 2025).
6
Done
₹9,000 a year सालाना ₹9,000
Check both on the PM-KISAN status page and your bank passbook.
Stuck? Getting PM-KISAN but not the state share? Complain on Sampark 181 with your PM-KISAN registration number.
Instalment missing? Complain.
PM-KISAN helpline 155261 / 011-24300606 or the grievance tab on pmkisan.gov.in; state share: Rajasthan Sampark 181 or the agriculture supervisor / tehsil.
