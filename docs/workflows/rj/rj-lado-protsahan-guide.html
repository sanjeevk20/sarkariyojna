Lado Protsahan: ₹1.5 lakh for a daughter in Rajasthan

लाडो प्रोत्साहन योजना: बेटी के लिए ₹1.5 लाख
₹1.5 lakh savings bond paid in 7 instalments by DBT, from birth to graduation (raised from ₹1 lakh in 2025)
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)All instalments received
At birth
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
  • Mother is a native of Rajasthan
  • Girl born in a government or approved hospital
  • Family from an economically weaker group (as reported)
Tip: Reported: the older Rajshree Yojana was merged into Lado Protsahan in March 2026. Check with the Anganwadi.
2
You
Keep these ready ये तैयार रखें
  • Mother's Jan Aadhaar with the girl added
  • Birth certificate and hospital discharge papers
  • Mother's bank account, Aadhaar-linked
Tip: Almost every Rajasthan service now starts from your family's Jan Aadhaar. Names must match Aadhaar. No Jan Aadhaar yet? See the Jan Aadhaar guide first.
3
Government
Registered at the hospital अस्पताल में पंजीकरण
The bond is registered from the hospital delivery record; ask the hospital or ASHA to confirm.
Stuck? No registration at discharge? Ask the Anganwadi worker or the block Women and Child Development office.
As she grows
4
Government
First instalments पहली किस्तें
  • ₹2,500 at birth
  • ₹2,500 on completing vaccinations (about 1 year)
5
Government
School-stage instalments पढ़ाई के दौरान किस्तें
  • ₹4,000 at Class 1 admission
  • ₹5,000 at Class 6
  • ₹11,000 at Class 10
  • ₹25,000 at Class 12
Tip: Since November 2025, girls in recognised private schools also qualify (reported). The school updates enrolment.
6
Done
₹1 lakh after graduation at 21 21 वर्ष पर स्नातक के बाद ₹1 लाख
The final ₹1 lakh is paid after graduation at age 21.
Stuck? An instalment missing? Ask the school or Anganwadi to check the enrolment record, then complain on Sampark 181.
Instalment missing? Complain.
Complain on Rajasthan Sampark 181 with the mother's Jan Aadhaar number, or to the district Women and Child Development office.
