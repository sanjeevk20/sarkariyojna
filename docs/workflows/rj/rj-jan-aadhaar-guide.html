Jan Aadhaar: your family's key to Rajasthan schemes

जन आधार: राजस्थान की योजनाओं की परिवार चाबी
One family ID (10 digits) and member IDs (11 digits) used for pensions, ration, health cover, scholarships and certificates
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Jan Aadhaar ready
Before you enrol
1
You
Know why it matters जानें क्यों ज़रूरी है
Jan Aadhaar replaced the old Bhamashah card. Pensions, ration, MAA health cover and most certificates check your details against Jan Aadhaar, so a wrong name or missing member blocks them.
2
You
Keep these ready ये तैयार रखें
  • Aadhaar of every family member
  • Photo of the woman head of family
  • Bank passbook or cancelled cheque
  • A mobile number that matches Aadhaar (for OTP)
Tip: If your mobile differs between Aadhaar and Jan Aadhaar, fix one first, or verify by fingerprint at e-Mitra.
3
You
Enrol online or at e-Mitra ऑनलाइन या ई-मित्र से नामांकन
Enrol with your SSO ID on the Jan Aadhaar portal, or at any e-Mitra kiosk. Keep the enrolment number.
Tip: Kiosk charges reported are about ₹30–50. Ask for a receipt.
After you enrol
4
Government
Verification सत्यापन
Your details are checked and the family ID is generated, reported as 7–15 working days.
Stuck? Still pending after 15 working days? Check status on the portal with your enrolment number, then complain on Sampark 181.
5
You
Keep it up to date इसे अपडेट रखें
Add a newborn or a bride, remove a member who died or left, and correct names to match Aadhaar, through SSO or e-Mitra. Do member eKYC when asked.
Stuck? Name mismatch between Aadhaar and Jan Aadhaar is a common reason pensions and ration stop. Fix it here first.
6
Done
Download and use your Jan Aadhaar जन आधार डाउनलोड करें और उपयोग करें
Download the e-card from the portal and quote the family ID in every scheme form.
Stuck? Complain.
Complain on Rajasthan Sampark 181 (toll-free) or sampark.rajasthan.gov.in with your enrolment number. Keep the complaint number.
