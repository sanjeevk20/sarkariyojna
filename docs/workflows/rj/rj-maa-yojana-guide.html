MAA Yojana: cashless treatment up to ₹25 lakh

मुख्यमंत्री आयुष्मान आरोग्य (मा) योजना
Cashless hospital treatment up to ₹25 lakh per family per year, plus ₹10 lakh accident cover
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Treated cashless
Before you need it
1
You
Check free or paid मुफ़्त या सशुल्क जांचें
  • Free: NFSA ration families, SECC 2011 families, small and marginal farmers, state contract workers
  • Others: about ₹850 per family per year
2
You
Register with Jan Aadhaar जन आधार से पंजीकरण
On sso.rajasthan.gov.in choose the MAA Yojana service, enter the Jan Aadhaar number, do Aadhaar eKYC and choose free or paid. Or go to e-Mitra.
Tip: Almost every Rajasthan service now starts from your family's Jan Aadhaar. Names must match Aadhaar. No Jan Aadhaar yet? See the Jan Aadhaar guide first.
3
You
Keep the family's eKYC done परिवार का ई-केवाईसी पूरा रखें
Aadhaar eKYC is needed for the head of family and members above 5 years.
Stuck? A member missing from Jan Aadhaar is not covered. Add them to Jan Aadhaar first.
At the hospital
4
Government
Cover becomes active बीमा सक्रिय होता है
Once registered (and the premium paid, if you are in the paid group), the family is covered for the policy year.
5
You
At an empanelled hospital सूचीबद्ध अस्पताल में
Show your Jan Aadhaar at the MAA help desk of an empanelled hospital. Treatment in covered packages should be cashless.
Stuck? Asked to pay for a covered package? Do not pay; ask the help desk for a written refusal and call Sampark 181.
6
Done
Cashless treatment कैशलेस इलाज
Keep the discharge summary. Check that no money was charged for covered items.
Refused or charged? Complain.
Complain on Rajasthan Sampark 181 with your Jan Aadhaar number and the hospital name, and to the scheme helpline shown on the MAA portal.
Register: SSO portal or e-Mitra · Complaints: Sampark 181
Free for eligible families · Premium and cover as reported
