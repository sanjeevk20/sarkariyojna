Namantaran: get land into your name in Rajasthan

नामांतरण: ज़मीन अपने नाम कराएं
Your name in the jamabandi (record of rights), needed for PM-KISAN, crop loans, insurance and selling the land

Before you apply
1
You
Know when you need it जानें कब ज़रूरी है
After a sale, gift, inheritance (death of the holder) or partition, the land is not yours on paper until namantaran is done.
2
You
Keep these ready ये तैयार रखें
  • Registered sale / gift deed, or death certificate and list of heirs
  • Current jamabandi copy (from Apna Khata)
  • Jan Aadhaar and Aadhaar of applicants
Tip: Almost every Rajasthan service now starts from your family's Jan Aadhaar. Names must match Aadhaar. No Jan Aadhaar yet? See the Jan Aadhaar guide first.
3
You
Apply online or at the tehsil ऑनलाइन या तहसील में आवेदन करें
Apply on Apna Khata / e-Dharti (apnakhata.rajasthan.gov.in) through e-Mitra, or give an application to the Patwari / tehsil. Keep the application or mutation number.
Tip: After a registered sale the registration office often starts the mutation itself. Ask for the mutation number at the sub-registrar.
After you apply
4
Government
Patwari report and public notice पटवारी रिपोर्ट और सार्वजनिक सूचना
The Patwari checks on the ground and a notice lets co-holders object. The Tehsildar / revenue officer then decides.
Stuck? Patwari asking for money to 'start' or 'pass' the file? That is a bribe. Report it to the ACB helpline 1064 before paying anything.
5
Government
Order and jamabandi update आदेश और जमाबंदी अपडेट
The officer approves the mutation and the jamabandi is updated online.
Stuck? Rejected or objected? Ask for the written order; you can appeal to the SDM.
6
Done
Check your name online ऑनलाइन नाम जांचें
Search your khasra / khata on Apna Khata and download the new jamabandi copy.
Tip: Now update PM-KISAN land details and your bank's crop-loan record.
Taking too long? Use your rights.
Ask the tehsil for the mutation status in writing, then complain on Rajasthan Sampark 181 with the mutation number. Bribe demand: ACB helpline 1064.
Records / status: apnakhata.rajasthan.gov.in · Complaints: Sampark 181 · Bribe demand: ACB 1064
