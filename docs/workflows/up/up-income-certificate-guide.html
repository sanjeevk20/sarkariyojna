Income Certificate (UP): How to get your aay praman patra

आय प्रमाण पत्र (उत्तर प्रदेश): कैसे बनवाएं
Needed for scholarships, pensions, fee waivers and most welfare schemes
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Certificate ready
Before you apply
1
You
Know who issues it जानें कौन बनाता है
The Tehsildar issues it after the Lekhpal checks your family income. Apply through e-District UP.
Tip: Apply in the name the scheme asks for. For a student, this is usually the parent's income.
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागज़ तैयार रखें
  • Aadhaar
  • Ration card or other address proof
  • Income proof: salary or pension slip; farmers and daily-wage earners give a self-declaration
  • Passport-size photo
3
You
Apply online or at a Jan Seva Kendra ऑनलाइन या जन सेवा केंद्र से आवेदन करें
Apply yourself on edistrict.up.gov.in or at a Jan Seva Kendra (CSC). Pay the fee and keep the application number.
Tip: Reported fee: about ₹15 if you apply online yourself, ₹30 at a Jan Seva Kendra. Do not pay more.
After you apply
4
Government
Lekhpal verifies लेखपाल जांच करते हैं
The Lekhpal checks your income, sometimes by visiting the village, and sends a report.
Stuck? Report delayed? Meet the Lekhpal with your application number. Wrong income written? Ask for re-verification before the Tehsildar signs.
5
Government
Tehsildar approves तहसीलदार स्वीकृति देते हैं
The Tehsildar approves the report and the certificate is digitally signed.
6
Done
Download your certificate प्रमाण पत्र डाउनलोड करें
Download it from e-District with your application number, or collect it at the CSC. Check your name, father's name and income.
Tip: Schemes often want a recent certificate (commonly within a year). Check what your scheme asks for.
Taking too long? Complain.
UP services have fixed time limits. If yours is crossed, complain on IGRS Jansunwai (jansunwai.up.nic.in) or the CM Helpline 1076 with your application number.
Apply / status: edistrict.up.gov.in or any Jan Seva Kendra · Complaints: jansunwai.up.nic.in, CM Helpline 1076
