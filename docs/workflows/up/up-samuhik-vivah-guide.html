Samuhik Vivah Yojana (UP): Help for your daughter's wedding

मुख्यमंत्री सामूहिक विवाह योजना: बेटी की शादी में सहायता
Reported ₹1 lakh per couple: ₹60,000 to the bride's account, gifts and the ceremony at a group wedding
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Assistance received
Before the wedding
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
  • Bride 18+, groom 21+
  • Family living in UP
  • Family income within the limit (reported ₹3 lakh; earlier ₹2 lakh)
  • Widows and divorced women can also apply
  • The wedding happens at a group ceremony organised by the district
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागज़ तैयार रखें
  • Aadhaar of bride and groom
  • Age proof of both
  • Income certificate
  • Bride's bank account (Aadhaar-linked)
  • Photos; caste certificate if asked
3
You
Register before the ceremony समारोह से पहले पंजीकरण
Register on cmsvy.upsdc.gov.in or through the block / municipal office, with Aadhaar eKYC of both. Keep the registration slip.
Tip: Registration is free. Nobody can sell you a 'seat' in the ceremony.
At and after the wedding
4
Government
Verification and list सत्यापन और सूची
The BDO or urban local body verifies, and the couple is added to the ceremony list.
Stuck? Name not on the list? Ask the block or municipal office which document is pending.
5
You
Attend the group wedding सामूहिक विवाह में शामिल हों
Come on the announced date with the bride's and groom's IDs. The marriage is solemnised at the venue.
6
Done
Assistance received सहायता मिली
Money reaches the bride's bank account by DBT; gifts are handed over at the venue.
Stuck? Money not received weeks later? Check the account is Aadhaar-linked, then contact the District Social Welfare Officer.
Payment missing? Complain.
Complain to the District Social Welfare Officer, on IGRS Jansunwai or the CM Helpline 1076 with the registration number.
Register: cmsvy.upsdc.gov.in · Amounts and income limit as reported; check the current notice
