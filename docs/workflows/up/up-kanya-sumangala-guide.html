Kanya Sumangala Yojana: ₹25,000 for your daughter in six stages

मुख्यमंत्री कन्या सुमंगला योजना: बेटी के लिए छह चरणों में ₹25,000
₹25,000 per girl, paid in six instalments from birth to college
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Instalment received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
  • Family living permanently in UP
  • Family income up to ₹3 lakh a year
  • Usually up to two daughters per family (twins counted specially)
  • Adopted daughters can also be covered
2
You
Know the six stages छह चरण जानें
  • Birth: ₹5,000
  • Full vaccination by age 1: ₹2,000
  • Class 1 admission: ₹3,000
  • Class 6 admission: ₹3,000
  • Class 9 admission: ₹5,000
  • Degree or diploma admission after Class 12: ₹7,000
Tip: Amounts as reported after the increase to ₹25,000. Check the portal for the current split.
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागज़ तैयार रखें
  • Girl's birth certificate
  • Parent's Aadhaar and photo
  • Income certificate or self-declaration
  • Bank account (Aadhaar-linked)
  • Stage proof: vaccination card or school admission slip
4
You
Apply on the scheme portal योजना पोर्टल पर आवेदन करें
Register on mksy.up.gov.in (citizen login), choose the stage, upload documents and submit.
Tip: Applying is free. Apply soon after each stage; late applications can be rejected.
After you apply (repeat at each stage)
5
Government
Verification सत्यापन
The BDO (village) or SDM (town) office verifies the application.
Stuck? Sent back for correction? Log in, fix the marked document and resubmit.
6
Government
District approval ज़िले से स्वीकृति
The District Probation Officer approves the instalment.
7
Done
Money in the account खाते में राशि
The instalment is paid by DBT. Log in again at the next stage to claim the next instalment.
Instalment stuck? Follow up.
Contact the District Probation Officer with your application number, or complain on IGRS Jansunwai / CM Helpline 1076. Women's helpline 181 can guide you.
Apply / status: mksy.up.gov.in · Amounts and rules as reported; check the current notice
