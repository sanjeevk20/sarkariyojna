Dakhil-Kharij (UP): Get your name into the khatauni

दाखिल-खारिज (उत्तर प्रदेश): खतौनी में अपना नाम दर्ज कराएं
Your name in the land record, needed for loans, PM-KISAN, sale and compensation
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Name recorded
Before you apply
1
You
Know when you need it जानें कब ज़रूरी है
After you buy land or receive it as a gift. Until mutation, the khatauni still shows the old owner.
Tip: Land passed on after a death is recorded through varasat (succession). Ask your Lekhpal for that separate entry.
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागज़ तैयार रखें
  • Registered sale or gift deed
  • Current khatauni copy (from upbhulekh.gov.in)
  • Aadhaar of buyer and seller
3
You
Start the mutation case दाखिल-खारिज मुकदमा शुरू कराएं
Apply at the tehsil or on the Board of Revenue vaad portal (vaad.up.nic.in). Pay the fee and keep the case number.
Stuck? No case number weeks after the registry? Go to the tehsil's dakhil-kharij counter with a copy of the deed.
After you apply
4
Government
Public notice for objections आपत्ति के लिए सार्वजनिक सूचना
A notice is issued so anyone with a claim can object, usually for 30 days.
Stuck? Someone objected (often a co-sharer)? The case becomes disputed and goes to a hearing before the Tehsildar. Bring the deed and witnesses.
5
Government
Lekhpal report लेखपाल की रिपोर्ट
The Lekhpal checks possession and the deed details and reports to the court.
6
Government
Tehsildar order तहसीलदार का आदेश
The Tehsildar orders mutation. Target: 45 days for undisputed cases, 90 days for disputed ones.
7
Done
Your name in the khatauni खतौनी में आपका नाम
Check the updated khatauni on upbhulekh.gov.in.
Stuck? Order rejected or wrong? You can usually appeal to the SDM within 30 days.
Case stuck past the deadline? Complain.
The state has ordered undisputed cases cleared in 45 days. Complain on IGRS Jansunwai or the CM Helpline 1076 with your case number and registry date.
