CM-YUVA: Interest-free loan up to ₹5 lakh to start a business

मुख्यमंत्री युवा उद्यमी विकास अभियान: व्यवसाय के लिए ₹5 लाख तक ब्याज-मुक्त ऋण
Loan up to ₹5 lakh without interest or collateral, plus a 10% margin-money subsidy (as reported)
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Loan received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
  • UP resident aged 21 to 40
  • At least Class 8 pass
  • Skill training under a government scheme helps (often given priority)
2
You
Plan your business अपने व्यवसाय की योजना बनाएं
Choose the activity and write a simple project report: what you will make or sell, machines or stock needed, and total cost.
Tip: The district industries centre (DIC) can help you shape the project report for free.
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागज़ तैयार रखें
  • Aadhaar, PAN, photo
  • Education and training certificates
  • Project report
  • Bank account details
4
You
Apply online ऑनलाइन आवेदन करें
Apply on the UP MSME portal (CM-YUVA, via msme1connect.up.gov.in) and choose a bank branch. Keep the application ID.
Tip: Applying is free. No agent can 'guarantee' a loan.
After you apply
5
Government
DIC scrutiny डीआईसी जांच
The District Industries Centre checks your application and forwards it to the bank.
Stuck? Returned by DIC? Fix the project report or documents and resubmit.
6
Government
Bank sanction बैंक स्वीकृति
The bank appraises the project and sanctions the loan. No collateral should be asked for under the scheme.
Stuck? Bank sitting on the file? Tell the DIC; banks have been told to speed up CM-YUVA loans.
7
Done
Loan received ऋण मिला
Money is released for your business. Repay on time to keep it interest-free and qualify for a larger second-phase loan.
Stuck with the bank or DIC? Escalate.
Contact the General Manager, District Industries Centre, use the MSME helpline shown on the portal, or complain on IGRS Jansunwai / CM Helpline 1076.
Apply: UP MSME portal (CM-YUVA) via msme1connect.up.gov.in · Loan terms as reported; check the current notice
