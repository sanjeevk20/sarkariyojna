UP Scholarship: Fee reimbursement and scholarship for students

उत्तर प्रदेश छात्रवृत्ति एवं शुल्क प्रतिपूर्ति
Scholarship and fee reimbursement paid into the student's bank account
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Money received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
  • Student domiciled in UP, in Class 9–10 (pre-matric) or Class 11 to college (post-matric)
  • Institute and course registered on the portal
  • Family income within the limit: about ₹2 to 2.5 lakh depending on category; the limit shown on the portal is final
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागज़ तैयार रखें
  • Aadhaar and an Aadhaar-seeded bank account in the student's name
  • Income certificate from the tehsil, in the parent's name
  • Caste certificate, if applying in a reserved category
  • Last marksheet, fee receipt, photo
Stuck? Private-employer salary letters or plain-paper declarations are not accepted as income proof. Get a tehsil income certificate.
3
You
Register and apply on the portal पोर्टल पर पंजीकरण और आवेदन
Do the One Time Registration (OTR) on scholarship.up.gov.in, then fill the fresh or renewal form before the deadline (usually September–October).
Tip: Deadlines are strict. Check the current dates on the portal's notice board.
4
You
Submit the printout to your institute संस्थान में प्रिंट जमा करें
Print the final form and give it with documents to your school or college if asked, and get a receipt.
After you apply
5
Government
Institute verifies संस्थान सत्यापन करता है
Your institute checks and forwards the application online, usually within a few days.
Stuck? Institute has not verified? Remind the scholarship clerk before the institute deadline.
6
Government
Department and bank checks विभाग और बैंक की जांच
The district office checks eligibility and the bank account is validated through PFMS.
Stuck? Error shown in status? Use the correction window on the portal; bank errors are fixed at your bank.
7
Done
Money in your account खाते में राशि
The scholarship and fee reimbursement are paid by DBT. Check status on the portal.
Not paid? Follow up.
Check the status page first, then contact the District Social Welfare / Backward Class / Minority Welfare Officer (by your category), or complain on IGRS Jansunwai.
Apply / status: scholarship.up.gov.in · Income limits and dates change each year; the portal is final
