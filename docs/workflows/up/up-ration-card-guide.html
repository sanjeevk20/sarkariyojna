Ration Card (UP): Get free grain under the food security law

राशन कार्ड (उत्तर प्रदेश): खाद्य सुरक्षा कानून के तहत मुफ़्त अनाज
Free grain every month from the fair price shop
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Ration received
Before you apply
1
You
Know the two card types कार्ड के दो प्रकार जानें
  • Antyodaya (AAY): poorest families, 35 kg grain per family a month
  • Patra Grihasthi: eligible households, 5 kg per person a month
Grain under these cards is free.
2
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता जांचें
Low-income families usually qualify. Families with an income-tax payer, a government job or large land or vehicles are usually excluded.
Tip: Search your name in the eligibility list on fcs.up.gov.in first; you may already be listed.
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागज़ तैयार रखें
  • Aadhaar of every family member
  • Photo of the head of family (the eldest woman is usually made head)
  • Bank account details
  • Income certificate and address proof
4
You
Apply आवेदन करें
Apply on fcs.up.gov.in, at a Jan Seva Kendra, or at the tehsil / town supply office. Keep the application number.
Tip: The form is free.
After you apply
5
Government
Verification सत्यापन
The Supply Inspector (town) or ADO / block official (village) checks your family and income.
Stuck? Rejected? Ask for the reason in writing; you can apply again or appeal to the District Supply Officer.
6
Government
Card issued कार्ड जारी
Your card number is generated and the family is attached to a fair price shop (kotedar).
7
Done
Collect ration every month हर महीने राशन लें
At the shop, a family member gives an Aadhaar fingerprint on the ePoS machine and collects the grain.
Stuck? Fingerprint fails or shopkeeper gives less? Ask for a receipt and complain to the supply office or CM Helpline 1076.
Shopkeeper cheating or card pending? Complain.
Complain to the District Supply Officer, on the helpline shown on fcs.up.gov.in, or the CM Helpline 1076.
