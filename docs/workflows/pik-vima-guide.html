Crop Insurance (PM Fasal Bima): How to insure your crop

पीक विमा (प्रधानमंत्री पीक विमा योजना): तुमच्या पिकाचा विमा कसा काढायचा
Compensation when natural calamity, pests or disease cut your crop yield
You do thisGovernment / insurer does this (you wait)Claim received
Before the deadline
1
You
Know what has changed काय बदलले ते जाणा
  • The ₹1 crop insurance ended in 2025. You now pay a small premium: about 2% of sum insured for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi, 5% for commercial / horticulture crops
  • Owner, tenant and sharecropper farmers growing a notified crop in a notified area can join
2
You
Get your AgriStack Farmer ID ॲग्रीस्टॅक शेतकरी ओळखपत्र मिळवा
A Farmer ID is mandatory. Register on mhfr.agristack.gov.in or at a CSC with Aadhaar and your 7/12.
Stuck? No Farmer ID? You cannot enrol. Do this weeks before the deadline.
3
You
Record your crop in e-Pik Pahani ई-पीक पाहणीत पिकाची नोंद करा
Register the crop you sowed on the e-Pik Pahani app for your survey / Gat number.
Stuck? If the crop in your insurance form does not match e-Pik Pahani, the policy can be cancelled and the premium lost.
4
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • 7/12 and 8A (tenants: lease / sharecropping agreement)
  • Aadhaar, bank passbook (account linked to Aadhaar)
  • Sowing declaration (crop, area)
  • Farmer ID
5
You
Enrol and pay premium before the deadline मुदतीपूर्वी नोंदणी करून हप्ता भरा
Enrol on pmfby.gov.in (Farmer Corner), the Crop Insurance app, at a CSC or at your bank. Pay your premium share. Keep the policy / receipt number.
Tip: Kharif enrolment usually closes around end of July. Check the current season's date. Loanee farmers: ask your bank whether you are enrolled.
After you enrol
6
You
Check your policy पॉलिसी तपासा
Check the policy on pmfby.gov.in with your receipt number: crop, area, survey number and bank must be correct.
Stuck? Wrong crop or area? Ask the CSC / bank / insurer to correct it before the correction deadline.
7
Govt / Insurer
Crop loss is assessed पीक नुकसानीचे मूल्यमापन
Under the revised scheme, compensation is mainly based on crop-cutting experiments in your revenue circle: if the average yield is below the threshold yield, insured farmers get a claim.
Tip: Damage on your own field? Inform the insurer / agriculture office quickly with photos and ask if an individual claim is allowed this season.
8
Done
Claim paid to your bank दावा रक्कम बँक खात्यात
Approved claims are paid directly into your Aadhaar-linked bank account. Check claim status on pmfby.gov.in.
Claim missing or wrong? Complain.
Raise a complaint on pmfby.gov.in or the official helpline listed there, and write to the Taluka Agriculture Officer / district agriculture office with your policy number. Take an acknowledgement (पोच).
Enrol / status: pmfby.gov.in · Farmer ID: mhfr.agristack.gov.in · Rates and dates change by season; check the current notification
