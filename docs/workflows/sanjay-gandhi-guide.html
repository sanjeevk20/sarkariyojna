Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Yojana: Monthly support for those without support

संजय गांधी निराधार अनुदान योजना: निराधारांना दरमहा अनुदान
Monthly financial support (about ₹1,500) paid into your bank account
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Money received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
You may be eligible if you are below 65, have lived in Maharashtra for many years, have very low family income, and are:
  • A widow, divorced or abandoned woman
  • A person with disability (40% or more)
  • Suffering from a serious illness (e.g. TB, cancer, HIV, leprosy)
  • An orphan child, transgender person, or another destitute category
Tip: Aged 65 or more? Ask about the Shravanbal / old-age pension schemes instead.
2
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Aadhaar, photo, ration card
  • Age proof and residence proof
  • Income certificate from the Tahsildar
  • Category proof: husband's death certificate (widow), disability certificate / UDID, or medical certificate from a government hospital
  • Bank passbook, account linked to Aadhaar
Stuck? No income certificate? Get it first on Aaple Sarkar. No disability certificate? Get it from the government (civil) hospital.
3
You
Apply (it is free) अर्ज करा (मोफत)
Choose any one:
  • Aaple Sarkar portal / Seva Kendra
  • Sanjay Gandhi Yojana branch at your Tahsil office, or ask your Talathi for help
Note your application number and keep the acknowledgement.
Tip: Applying is free. Never pay an agent to "get it sanctioned".
After you apply
4
Government
Papers are checked कागदपत्रांची तपासणी
The Tahsil office checks your papers. The Talathi may do a local inquiry about your situation and income.
Stuck? Asked for more papers? Submit them quickly with your application number and take an acknowledgement.
5
Government
Taluka committee approves तालुका समितीची मंजुरी
The taluka-level Sanjay Gandhi Yojana committee meets regularly and approves eligible applications.
Stuck? Rejected? Ask for the reason in writing. Fix the missing proof and apply again.
6
Done
Money every month दरमहा अनुदान जमा
The amount comes by DBT into your Aadhaar-linked bank account every month.
Stuck? Approved but no money? Check the bank account is Aadhaar-seeded with DBT on and active.
7
You
Keep it running अनुदान चालू ठेवा
  • Submit your life certificate (हयातीचा दाखला) every year when asked
  • Tell the office about changes (address, bank, remarriage, etc.)
  • At 65, ask to shift to the old-age pension scheme
Stuck? Money suddenly stopped? Usually the life certificate was not submitted or the bank link changed.
Pending too long? Follow up.
Give a written application to the Tahsildar / Naib Tahsildar (Sanjay Gandhi Yojana) with your application number and take an acknowledgement (पोच). You can also raise a grievance on Aaple Sarkar.
Check status: Aaple Sarkar (application number) or Tahsil office
