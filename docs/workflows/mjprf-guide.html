Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Research Fellowship: PhD support from MahaJyoti

महात्मा ज्योतिबा फुले संशोधन अधिछात्रवृत्ती: महाज्योतीकडून पीएचडीसाठी मदत
Monthly fellowship for up to 5 years for full-time PhD scholars from OBC, VJ / NT and SBC
You do thisMahaJyoti / University does this (you wait)Fellowship received
Before you apply
1
You
Pick the right MahaJyoti scheme योग्य महाज्योती योजना निवडा
MahaJyoti (Nagpur) runs several schemes for OBC, VJ / NT and SBC students:
  • 10th passed, Science → free tablet + internet + CET / JEE / NEET coaching
  • UG / PG students → online course fee support
  • Full-time PhD → MJPRF fellowship. This guide.
2
You
Check MJPRF eligibility पात्रता तपासा
  • OBC, VJ / NT or SBC, non-creamy layer, Maharashtra domicile
  • Post-graduate degree, admitted to a full-time PhD (not distance mode)
  • Within the age limit in the notice (about 45 in recent rounds)
  • Not already holding UGC / CSIR NET-JRF or another fellowship
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Caste certificate and valid Non-Creamy Layer certificate
  • Domicile certificate
  • PG marksheets and degree
  • PhD registration / admission letter, guide's consent, research proposal
  • Aadhaar, Aadhaar-linked bank account, undertaking in the given format
Stuck? NCL expired or no caste validity? Fix these first. See the NCL and caste validity guides.
4
You
Apply when the notice opens जाहिरात आल्यावर अर्ज करा
Watch mahajyoti.org.in for the fellowship notice. Apply online on MahaJyoti's PhD portal within the dates. Note your registration ID.
Tip: Deadlines are strict and rounds are once a year. Keep documents ready before the notice comes.
After you apply
5
MahaJyoti / University
Scrutiny and selection list छाननी व निवड यादी
MahaJyoti checks applications and publishes lists (eligible / selected / errors) on its website. Document verification may follow.
Stuck? Name in the error / pending list? Read the remark and submit the missing document within the given time.
6
You
Join and submit undertaking रुजू होऊन हमीपत्र द्या
Submit the joining report through your university / research centre and the signed undertaking. If you are employed, you may need to give a resignation / relieving letter.
7
Done
Fellowship paid, keep reporting अधिछात्रवृत्ती जमा, अहवाल देत राहा
The fellowship (JRF level first, then SRF) is paid into your Aadhaar-linked bank account, usually every six months, based on your progress report and utilisation certificate, for up to 5 years.
Stuck? Payment delayed? Usually a progress report or utilisation certificate was not sent through the university on time.
Payment or selection stuck? Follow up.
Write to MahaJyoti, Nagpur with your registration ID and copies of submitted reports, and take an acknowledgement. Ask your university research cell to forward pending reports.
Notices and lists: mahajyoti.org.in · Amounts and age limits change by notice; read the current one
Applying is free · Use only official websites
