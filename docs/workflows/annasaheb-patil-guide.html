Annasaheb Patil Loan Scheme: Interest-free business loan

अण्णासाहेब पाटील कर्ज योजना: व्यवसायासाठी बिनव्याजी कर्ज
Take a bank loan for your business; the corporation refunds the interest if you pay EMIs on time
You do thisCorporation / bank does this (you wait)Interest refunded
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
  • Resident of Maharashtra
  • From an economically backward family of a community not covered by another state corporation (e.g. Maratha and other open-category communities)
  • Within the age limit (adult, working age)
  • Family income up to about ₹8 lakh a year
Tip: Individual loans and group loans (for farmer groups / SHGs) are both available.
2
You
Plan your business व्यवसायाची योजना करा
Decide the business (farm-related, manufacturing, service or trade) and how much loan you need. Prepare a simple project report: cost, income, EMI you can pay.
Tip: A clear project report is what convinces the bank. Ask a CA or the district office for a format.
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Aadhaar, photo, mobile linked to Aadhaar
  • Income certificate or ITR
  • Domicile / residence proof, age proof
  • Community proof (e.g. school leaving certificate / caste certificate)
  • Project report, bank details
Stuck? No income or domicile certificate? Get them first on Aaple Sarkar.
4
You
Register and get the Letter of Intent नोंदणी करून LOI मिळवा
Register on the corporation's portal (udyog.mahaswayam.gov.in), fill your details, upload documents and apply for the Letter of Intent (LOI) / eligibility certificate.
Tip: Registration is free. Never pay an agent to "get the loan sanctioned".
After you apply
5
Corporation / Bank
Corporation issues LOI महामंडळाकडून LOI
The corporation checks your documents and issues the LOI, which tells the bank you are eligible for interest refund.
Stuck? Application sent back? Usually a document is unclear or income proof is missing. Re-upload and resubmit.
6
Corporation / Bank
Bank sanctions the loan बँकेकडून कर्ज मंजूर
Take the LOI and project report to a bank. The bank decides and sanctions the loan. Upload the sanction letter on the portal.
Stuck? Bank refused? The corporation does not force banks. Try another bank, improve the project report, check your credit score, or ask the district coordinator for help.
7
You
Pay every EMI on time प्रत्येक हप्ता वेळेवर भरा
Pay EMIs regularly. Upload your loan account statement on the portal to claim the interest refund.
Stuck? Missed or late EMIs can stop the interest refund for that period.
8
Done
Interest refunded व्याज परतावा मिळतो
The corporation refunds the interest (up to a set limit, for a set number of years) for EMIs paid on time. Your loan becomes almost interest-free.
Stuck with corporation or bank? Escalate.
For LOI or refund delays, contact the corporation's district coordinator / office in writing. For unfair bank refusal, use the bank's grievance channel or the district Lead Bank Manager.
Portal: udyog.mahaswayam.gov.in · Keep EMI receipts and statements safe
Registration is free · Use only .gov.in websites
