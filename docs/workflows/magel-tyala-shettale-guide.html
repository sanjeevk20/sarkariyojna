Magel Tyala Shettale: Grant to dig a farm pond

मागेल त्याला शेततळे: शेततळे खोदण्यासाठी अनुदान
Up to ₹75,000 (by pond size) to store rainwater for protective irrigation
You do thisAgriculture office does this (you wait)Grant received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
  • Farmer in Maharashtra with at least 0.60 hectare of land in your own name
  • You have not received a farm-pond grant before under any scheme (including group farm ponds)
  • Open to farmers of all categories
Tip: It now runs as the farm-pond part of the Mukhyamantri Shashwat Krushi Sinchan Yojana on MahaDBT.
2
You
Choose the pond size शेततळ्याचा आकार निवडा
Pond sizes range from about 15 × 15 m to 34 × 34 m, around 3 m deep, with or without inlet / outlet. The grant depends on the size: from about ₹14,000 up to ₹75,000.
Tip: Choose a low-lying spot where rainwater collects. The Krushi Sahayak checks if the site is suitable.
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • 7/12 and 8A extracts in your name
  • Aadhaar, Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Self-declaration that you have not taken a farm-pond grant before
  • Caste certificate, if applying in a reserved category
Stuck? Land still in a parent's name? Get the ferfar done first. See the Ferfar guide.
4
You
Apply on MahaDBT महाडीबीटीवर अर्ज करा
Log in on mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in as a Farmer, choose farm pond (शेततळे) under the agriculture schemes, select the size, upload documents and submit. Note your application ID.
Tip: From 2025-26 selection is first come, first served. Apply as soon as the window opens. Applying is free.
After you apply
5
Agriculture office
Pre-approval and site marking पूर्वसंमती व आखणी
If selected, you get pre-approval (पूर्वसंमती). The Krushi Sahayak checks the site and marks the pond layout.
Stuck? Do not start digging before pre-approval and marking. Ponds dug earlier are usually not paid.
6
You
Dig the pond, keep proof शेततळे खोदा, पुरावे ठेवा
Dig to the approved size within the time given. Take geo-tagged photos before, during and after digging and upload them on MahaDBT.
Stuck? Pond smaller than approved? The grant is cut to the size actually dug. Measure before you finish.
7
Agriculture office
Measurement and inspection मोजमाप व तपासणी
The agriculture office measures the pond and checks the photos.
8
Done
Grant paid to your bank अनुदान बँक खात्यात
The grant is paid by DBT into your Aadhaar-linked bank account.
Tip: Want plastic lining for the pond? It is a separate grant. SC / ST farmers: see Krushi Swavalamban / Birsa Munda; others ask about the horticulture / lining scheme.
Stuck for long? Escalate.
Write to the Taluka Agriculture Officer with your MahaDBT application ID and take an acknowledgement (पोच). You can also raise a grievance on MahaDBT.
Apply: mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in → Farmer login → farm pond · Amounts by pond size; check the current notice
