Non-Creamy Layer Certificate: How to get it on Aaple Sarkar

नॉन-क्रिमीलेयर प्रमाणपत्र: आपले सरकार वरून कसे मिळवायचे
Needed by OBC, VJ / NT and SBC students and job seekers to use reservation
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Certificate ready
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it पात्रता तपासा
You can apply if:
  • You belong to OBC, VJ / NT or SBC
  • Your family income is below ₹8 lakh a year
  • Your parents do not hold high posts (e.g. Class-I officers)
Tip: Check which income counts. Rules may exclude salary or farm income. Ask before you assume you are not eligible.
2
You
Get your caste certificate first आधी जातीचा दाखला मिळवा
The NCL certificate is issued only if you already have a caste certificate. If you don't have one, get it first.
Stuck? No caste certificate yet? See the Caste Certificate guide. Finding old proof takes time, so start early.
3
You
Keep these papers ready कागदपत्रे तयार ठेवा
  • Your caste certificate
  • Parents' income proof for the last 3 years: income certificate / ITR / Form 16
  • Parents' job or occupation details
  • Aadhaar, ration card / address proof
  • Self-declaration, photo
4
You
Apply अर्ज करा
Choose any one:
  • aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in → Revenue Department → Non-Creamy Layer Certificate
  • Nearest Aaple Sarkar Seva Kendra, or a camp at your college
Note your Application ID and the time limit (days) shown.
Tip: For central government jobs or admissions you need the central-format NCL. Tell the office which one you need.
After you apply
5
Government
Your papers are checked कागदपत्रांची तपासणी
The Tahsil / SDO office checks your caste certificate and 3 years of family income.
Stuck? Status says sent back? Usually one year's income proof is missing or unclear. Upload it and resubmit. No visit needed.
6
Government
SDO approves उपविभागीय अधिकाऱ्यांची मंजुरी
The Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) approves and signs digitally.
7
Done
Download your certificate प्रमाणपत्र डाउनलोड करा
Log in to Aaple Sarkar and download it. Check the validity date printed on it.
Stuck? Unlike the caste certificate, NCL expires. Note the date and renew before admission or job forms.
Late? It's your right.
If the time limit has passed, you can file a First Appeal on Aaple Sarkar with your Application ID, under the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015.
