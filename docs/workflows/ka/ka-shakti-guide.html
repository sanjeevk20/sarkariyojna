Shakti: Free bus travel for women in Karnataka

ಶಕ್ತಿ ಯೋಜನೆ: ಮಹಿಳೆಯರಿಗೆ ಉಚಿತ ಬಸ್ ಪ್ರಯಾಣ
Free travel in non-AC government buses within Karnataka
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Free ride
Before you travel
1
You
Check if you can use it ಅರ್ಹತೆ ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಿ
  • Women and transgender persons living in Karnataka
  • Ordinary and express non-AC buses of KSRTC, BMTC, KKRTC and NWKRTC
  • Travel within Karnataka only; AC and luxury buses are not free
2
You
Carry a valid ID ಮಾನ್ಯ ಗುರುತಿನ ಚೀಟಿ ಇಟ್ಟುಕೊಳ್ಳಿ
Carry a government photo ID with a Karnataka address (Aadhaar, voter ID and similar), or your Shakti smart card if one has been issued to you through Seva Sindhu.
Tip: A photocopy or phone photo may be refused. Keep the original ID or DigiLocker copy handy.
On the bus
3
You
Board and show your ID ಬಸ್ ಹತ್ತಿ ಗುರುತಿನ ಚೀಟಿ ತೋರಿಸಿ
Tell the conductor you are travelling under Shakti and show your ID or card.
4
Government
Zero-value ticket ಶೂನ್ಯ ಮೌಲ್ಯದ ಟಿಕೆಟ್
The conductor gives a zero-value 'Shakti' ticket. Keep it until you get off.
Stuck? Conductor refuses or asks for money? Note the bus number, time and route.
5
Done
Free ride ಉಚಿತ ಪ್ರಯಾಣ
You travel free. Interstate and AC journeys are charged as usual.
Refused a free ticket? Complain.
Complain to the corporation's depot or helpline (numbers are printed in the bus), or on IPGRS, with the bus number and time.
Smart card: sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in · Covered buses as reported; check the transport department notice
The ride is free; never pay for a 'Shakti pass'
