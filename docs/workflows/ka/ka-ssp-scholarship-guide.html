Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it ಅರ್ಹತೆ ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಿ
  • Karnataka student in a recognised school or college
  • Mainly for SC, ST, OBC and minority students; each department sets its own income limit
  • Income limits commonly reported between ₹1 lakh and ₹2.5 lakh by category; the portal is final
2
You
Keep these ready ಇವುಗಳನ್ನು ಸಿದ್ಧವಾಗಿಡಿ
  • Student's and parents' Aadhaar
  • Caste and income certificate RD number (Nadakacheri)
  • SSLC register number
  • Aadhaar-seeded bank account in the student's name
  • Kutumba family ID
Stuck? No RD number? Get the caste and income certificate first. See the Caste and income certificate (Karnataka) guide.
3
You
Register and apply ನೋಂದಾಯಿಸಿ ಅರ್ಜಿ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಿ
On ssp.karnataka.gov.in, create an account with Aadhaar eKYC, then apply for pre-matric or post-matric before your department's deadline.
Tip: Deadlines differ by department (Social Welfare, Backward Classes, Minorities, Tribal Welfare). Check yours on the portal.
4
You
Give documents to your institution ಸಂಸ್ಥೆಗೆ ದಾಖಲೆ ನೀಡಿ
Submit the printout and fee receipt to your school or college scholarship section if asked.
After you apply
5
Government
Institution verifies ಸಂಸ್ಥೆ ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸುತ್ತದೆ
Your institution checks bonafide status, fees and marks online.
Stuck? Not verified by the college? Remind the scholarship clerk before the institution deadline.
6
Government
Department approves ಇಲಾಖೆ ಅನುಮೋದನೆ
The department checks eligibility and approves the payment.
7
Done
Money in your account ಖಾತೆಗೆ ಹಣ
Paid by DBT to the Aadhaar-seeded account. Track status on the SSP portal.
Stuck? Payment failed? Usually an Aadhaar-seeding or NPCI-mapping problem. Fix it at the bank.
Not paid? Follow up.
Contact your department's taluk / district office (Social Welfare, Backward Classes, Minorities or Tribal Welfare), or complain on IPGRS.
Apply / status: ssp.karnataka.gov.in · Income limits and dates change each year; the portal is final
Applying is free · Use only official websites
