Gruha Lakshmi: ₹2,000 a month for the woman head of the family

ಗೃಹಲಕ್ಷ್ಮಿ: ಕುಟುಂಬದ ಯಜಮಾನಿಗೆ ತಿಂಗಳಿಗೆ ₹2,000
₹2,000 a month by DBT to one woman per family
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Money received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it ಅರ್ಹತೆ ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಿ
  • You are the woman head of the family on an AAY, BPL or APL ration card
  • Neither you nor your husband is a government employee or pays income tax / files GST returns
2
You
Keep these ready ಇವುಗಳನ್ನು ಸಿದ್ಧವಾಗಿಡಿ
  • Ration card number
  • Aadhaar of you and your husband
  • Bank account linked to Aadhaar with DBT on
  • Mobile linked to Aadhaar
3
You
Apply ಅರ್ಜಿ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಿ
Apply on Seva Sindhu or at a Grama One, Karnataka One or Bapuji Seva Kendra. Keep the acknowledgement number.
Tip: Applying is free. Never share your OTP or bank details with anyone who says they can 'release' a payment.
After you apply
4
Government
Verification ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆ
The department checks the ration card, Aadhaar and tax records. A biometric re-verification drive was reported in 2026.
Stuck? Asked to re-verify? Do it at the centre or during the door-to-door visit, or payments may stop.
5
Done
₹2,000 every month ಪ್ರತಿ ತಿಂಗಳು ₹2,000
Money arrives by DBT, often in the first half of the month. Check status on Seva Sindhu with your acknowledgement number.
Stuck? Not received? Usually Aadhaar is not seeded to the active account or DBT is off. Fix it at the bank, then check status again.
Still not paid? Ask for help.
Call the guarantee schemes helpline 1902, visit the taluk Women and Child Development office, or complain on IPGRS.
Apply / status: sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in, Grama One, Karnataka One · Helpline: 1902
Applying is free · Never share your OTP
