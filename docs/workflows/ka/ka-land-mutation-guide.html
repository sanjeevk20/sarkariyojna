Land Mutation (Karnataka): Get your name on the RTC (pahani)

Your name in the land record, needed for crop loans, PM-KISAN, sale and compensation

At registration
1
You
Register the sale or gift deed ಕ್ರಯ ಅಥವಾ ದಾನ ಪತ್ರ ನೋಂದಾಯಿಸಿ
Register at the Sub-Registrar office (Kaveri 2.0). Make sure names and survey / hissa numbers match the RTC exactly.
Stuck? A spelling or survey-number mismatch can make automatic mutation fail silently. Check the deed against the RTC before you sign.
2
Government
Mutation starts automatically ಮ್ಯುಟೇಶನ್ ಸ್ವಯಂಚಾಲಿತವಾಗಿ ಆರಂಭ
Registration sends a J-slip from Kaveri to Bhoomi, which opens a mutation entry (MR).
Tip: For land you inherit, there is no deed. Ask the Village Accountant or Nadakacheri about mutation by succession.
3
You
Watch for the MR entry MR ನಮೂದನ್ನು ಗಮನಿಸಿ
On landrecords.karnataka.gov.in (View RTC and MR), search by district, taluk, hobli, village and survey number.
Stuck? No MR entry two weeks after registration? Go to the taluk Bhoomi centre with the deed. The system does not alert you.
After registration
4
Government
Notice and objection period ನೋಟಿಸ್ ಮತ್ತು ಆಕ್ಷೇಪಣೆ ಅವಧಿ
Notice is issued to the parties. If nobody objects, the mutation is approved after the notice period.
Stuck? Someone objected? The case becomes disputed and goes to the revenue officer for a hearing. Bring the deed and witnesses.
5
Government
Mutation approved ಮ್ಯುಟೇಶನ್ ಅನುಮೋದನೆ
The revenue officer certifies the mutation and Bhoomi updates the owner column.
6
Done
Your name on the RTC ಪಹಣಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಮ್ಮ ಹೆಸರು
Download the updated RTC and mutation extract from Bhoomi.
Stuck? Order wrong or rejected? An appeal usually lies with the Assistant Commissioner. Ask the taluk office for the time limit.
Stuck for weeks? Escalate.
Visit the Tahsildar with the registration number and RTC copy, or complain on IPGRS.
