Caste and Income Certificate (Karnataka): Get it from Nadakacheri

ಜಾತಿ ಮತ್ತು ಆದಾಯ ಪ್ರಮಾಣ ಪತ್ರ: ನಾಡಕಚೇರಿಯಿಂದ ಪಡೆಯಿರಿ
Needed for scholarships, reservation, fee concessions and most welfare schemes
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Certificate ready
Before you apply
1
You
Know where to apply ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಅರ್ಜಿ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಬೇಕು
Apply through Nadakacheri (Atalji Janasnehi Kendra): online on nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in, or at a Nadakacheri, Grama One or Karnataka One centre.
Tip: Students usually need a combined caste and income certificate. Ask your college which form it needs.
2
You
Keep these papers ready ದಾಖಲೆಗಳನ್ನು ಸಿದ್ಧವಾಗಿಡಿ
  • Aadhaar
  • Ration card or other address proof
  • Income proof (salary slip, or a self-declaration for farmers and daily-wage workers)
  • A family member's caste certificate, if any
  • Registered mobile number
3
You
Submit and pay the fee ಅರ್ಜಿ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಿ ಶುಲ್ಕ ಪಾವತಿಸಿ
Submit the form and pay the small fee. Keep the application number.
Tip: Reported fee: about ₹15 for an income certificate. Do not pay agents more.
After you apply
4
Government
Village Accountant and Revenue Inspector check ಗ್ರಾಮ ಲೆಕ್ಕಾಧಿಕಾರಿ ಮತ್ತು ಕಂದಾಯ ನಿರೀಕ್ಷಕರ ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆ
The Village Accountant and Revenue Inspector verify your caste, residence and income and report to the taluk office.
Stuck? Report stuck? Meet the Village Accountant with your application number. Income written wrongly? Ask for re-verification before it is signed.
5
Government
Taluk office signs ತಾಲೂಕು ಕಚೇರಿಯಿಂದ ಸಹಿ
The Deputy Tahsildar / Tahsildar approves and the certificate is digitally signed with an RD number.
6
Done
Download or collect it ಡೌನ್‌ಲೋಡ್ ಮಾಡಿ ಅಥವಾ ಪಡೆಯಿರಿ
Download it with your application number, or collect it at the centre. Anyone can verify it online with the RD number.
Tip: Income certificates are reported to be valid for 12 months. Scholarships ask for the RD number, so note it down.
Taking too long? Complain.
Certificates have time limits under Sakala. If yours is crossed, complain on IPGRS (ipgrs.karnataka.gov.in) or at the taluk office with your application number.
Apply / status: nadakacheri.karnataka.gov.in, Grama One, Karnataka One · Complaints: ipgrs.karnataka.gov.in
