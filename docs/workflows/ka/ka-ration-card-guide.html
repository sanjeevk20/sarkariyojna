Ration Card and Anna Bhagya: 10 kg rice per person

ಪಡಿತರ ಚೀಟಿ ಮತ್ತು ಅನ್ನಭಾಗ್ಯ: ಪ್ರತಿ ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿಗೆ 10 ಕೆಜಿ ಅಕ್ಕಿ
10 kg food grain per person a month for BPL (PHH) and Antyodaya families
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Ration received
Before you apply
1
You
Already have a BPL or AAY card? ಈಗಾಗಲೇ BPL ಅಥವಾ AAY ಚೀಟಿ ಇದೆಯೇ?
Then you need no separate application for Anna Bhagya. Collect rice at your fair price shop.
2
You
Check if you can get a BPL card BPL ಚೀಟಿಗೆ ಅರ್ಹತೆ ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಿ
  • Permanent Karnataka resident, with no ration card anywhere else
  • No government employee or income-tax payer in the family
  • No four-wheeler (livelihood vehicles like taxis and tractors are exceptions)
  • Village: not more than 3 hectares of dry land; town: no pucca house over 1,000 sq ft
3
You
Keep these papers ready ದಾಖಲೆಗಳನ್ನು ಸಿದ್ಧವಾಗಿಡಿ
  • Aadhaar of every family member
  • Income certificate (Nadakacheri)
  • Address proof, photo of the head of family
4
You
Apply ಅರ್ಜಿ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಿ
Apply for a BPL / AAY card through the Ahara department at a Grama One, Karnataka One or Bapuji Seva Kendra when applications are open. Keep the acknowledgement.
Tip: New-card windows open and close; ask the centre or the taluk food office whether applications are being accepted now.
After you apply
5
Government
Verification ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆ
The Food Inspector / taluk food office checks your family and income.
Stuck? Rejected? Ask for the reason in writing and appeal to the Deputy Director, Food of your district.
6
Government
Card issued ಚೀಟಿ ವಿತರಣೆ
The card is approved and the family is attached to a fair price shop.
7
Done
Collect rice every month ಪ್ರತಿ ತಿಂಗಳು ಅಕ್ಕಿ ಪಡೆಯಿರಿ
A member gives a fingerprint at the shop and collects grain for every person on the card.
Stuck? Shop gives less or demands money? Ask for a receipt and call the food helpline 1967.
Shortage or card pending? Complain.
Call the food helpline 1967, contact the taluk food office, or complain on IPGRS.
