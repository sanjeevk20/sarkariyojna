Gruha Jyothi: Free electricity up to 200 units a month

ಗೃಹಜ್ಯೋತಿ: ತಿಂಗಳಿಗೆ 200 ಯೂನಿಟ್‌ವರೆಗೆ ಉಚಿತ ವಿದ್ಯುತ್
Zero bill for units within your entitlement, up to 200 units a month
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Zero bill
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it ಅರ್ಹತೆ ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಿ
  • A domestic (home) connection in Karnataka; commercial connections are excluded
  • One meter per household
  • Your average use must be under 200 units a month
2
You
Know your free units ನಿಮ್ಮ ಉಚಿತ ಯೂನಿಟ್ ತಿಳಿಯಿರಿ
Your free limit is based on your average monthly use in the past year plus about 10%. Very low users were reported to get a fixed top-up instead.
Tip: Units above your limit are billed. Keep use near your average to keep a zero bill.
3
You
Keep your bill ready ನಿಮ್ಮ ಬಿಲ್ ಸಿದ್ಧವಾಗಿಡಿ
You need the account ID / customer ID from your electricity bill, and Aadhaar. Tenants can apply with a rent agreement or address proof.
After you apply
4
You
Apply ಅರ್ಜಿ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಿ
Apply on Seva Sindhu or at a Grama One / Karnataka One / Bangalore One centre. Keep the acknowledgement.
Tip: Applying is free.
5
Government
Linked to your connection ನಿಮ್ಮ ಸಂಪರ್ಕಕ್ಕೆ ಜೋಡಣೆ
Your ESCOM (BESCOM, MESCOM, HESCOM, GESCOM or CESC) links the scheme to your account.
6
Done
Zero bill ಶೂನ್ಯ ಬಿಲ್
Your bill shows the Gruha Jyothi benefit and ₹0 when you stay within your limit.
Stuck? Still getting a full bill? Check that the application is linked to the right account ID; ask the ESCOM sub-division office.
Wrong bill? Complain.
Complain to your ESCOM sub-division office or helpline 1912, or on IPGRS.
