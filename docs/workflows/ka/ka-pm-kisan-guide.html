PM-KISAN in Karnataka: Register through FRUITS

ಕರ್ನಾಟಕದಲ್ಲಿ ಪಿಎಂ-ಕಿಸಾನ್: ಫ್ರೂಟ್ಸ್ ಮೂಲಕ ನೋಂದಣಿ
₹2,000 into your bank account, three times a year
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Money received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it ಅರ್ಹತೆ ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಿ
Your name must be on the RTC (pahani). You cannot get it if someone in the family pays income tax, holds a government job, or is a practising doctor, lawyer, CA or engineer.
Stuck? Land still in a parent's name? Get the mutation done first. See the Land mutation (Karnataka) guide.
2
You
Get your FRUITS ID ಫ್ರೂಟ್ಸ್ ಐಡಿ ಪಡೆಯಿರಿ
Karnataka uses FRUITS, a farmer database linked to Bhoomi, to match your Aadhaar name with the RTC. Register at the Raitha Samparka Kendra or on fruits.karnataka.gov.in.
Tip: Your FRUITS ID is also used for crop insurance, input subsidies and crop loans. Keep it handy.
3
You
Keep these papers ready ದಾಖಲೆಗಳನ್ನು ಸಿದ್ಧವಾಗಿಡಿ
  • RTC (pahani)
  • Aadhaar
  • Bank passbook (account linked to Aadhaar)
  • Mobile linked to Aadhaar
4
You
Register for PM-KISAN ಪಿಎಂ-ಕಿಸಾನ್‌ಗೆ ನೋಂದಾಯಿಸಿ
Register on pmkisan.gov.in, through Seva Sindhu, or at the Raitha Samparka Kendra. Note your registration number.
After you apply
5
Government
Land and name verification ಭೂಮಿ ಮತ್ತು ಹೆಸರಿನ ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆ
FRUITS compares the RTC owner name with your Aadhaar name; the state then approves you.
Stuck? Name mismatch between Aadhaar and RTC? Correct one of them (Aadhaar centre or taluk office), then ask the Raitha Samparka Kendra to re-verify.
6
You
Do eKYC ಇ-ಕೆವೈಸಿ ಮಾಡಿ
Complete eKYC by OTP on pmkisan.gov.in, face authentication in the PM-KISAN app, or fingerprint at a CSC.
Stuck? Without eKYC, payments stop.
7
Government
Payment processed ಪಾವತಿ ಪ್ರಕ್ರಿಯೆ
Status shows RFT signed by State Govt, then FTO generated. Just wait.
8
Done
₹2,000 in your account ಖಾತೆಗೆ ₹2,000
You get an SMS. This repeats every four months while eKYC, land seeding and bank linking stay correct.
Instalment missing? Complain.
Check status on pmkisan.gov.in, then contact the Raitha Samparka Kendra / Assistant Director of Agriculture, or the PM-KISAN helpline 155261 / 011-24300606.
