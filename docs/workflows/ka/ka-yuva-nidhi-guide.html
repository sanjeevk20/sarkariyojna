Yuva Nidhi: Monthly allowance for unemployed graduates

ಯುವನಿಧಿ: ನಿರುದ್ಯೋಗಿ ಪದವೀಧರರಿಗೆ ಮಾಸಿಕ ಭತ್ಯೆ
₹3,000 a month for graduates, ₹1,500 for diploma holders, for up to two years
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Allowance received
Before you apply
1
You
Check if you can get it ಅರ್ಹತೆ ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸಿ
  • Karnataka domicile, graduate or diploma holder
  • Unemployed for six months after passing
  • Not in higher studies, not getting apprenticeship stipend, not self-employed with a government-scheme loan, no government job
2
You
Keep these ready ಇವುಗಳನ್ನು ಸಿದ್ಧವಾಗಿಡಿ
  • Aadhaar and an Aadhaar-seeded bank account
  • Degree or diploma certificate and marks cards
  • Domicile proof (study in Karnataka or residence)
3
You
Apply on Seva Sindhu ಸೇವಾ ಸಿಂಧುವಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಅರ್ಜಿ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಿ
Register on Seva Sindhu (Yuva Nidhi), upload documents and sign the self-declaration that you are unemployed.
Stuck? False declarations can lead to recovery and penalty. Tell the department as soon as you get a job.
After you apply
4
Government
Verification ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆ
The Skill Development department checks your degree (including on the NAD academic database) and eligibility.
Stuck? Degree not found on NAD? Ask your university to upload it, then inform the district employment office.
5
You
Confirm every month ಪ್ರತಿ ತಿಂಗಳು ದೃಢೀಕರಿಸಿ
Keep confirming that you are still unemployed, as the portal asks, so the allowance continues.
6
Done
Allowance in your account ಖಾತೆಗೆ ಭತ್ಯೆ
Paid by DBT until you get a job or for a maximum of two years.
Not paid? Ask for help.
Contact the district employment exchange / Skill Development office, call 1902, or complain on IPGRS.
Apply: sevasindhugs.karnataka.gov.in · Helpline: 1902 · Rules as at launch; check the current notice
