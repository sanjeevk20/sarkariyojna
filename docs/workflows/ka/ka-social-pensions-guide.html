Social Security Pensions (Karnataka): Old-age and widow pension

ಸಾಮಾಜಿಕ ಭದ್ರತಾ ಪಿಂಚಣಿ: ವೃದ್ಧಾಪ್ಯ ಮತ್ತು ವಿಧವಾ ವೇತನ
Monthly pension into your bank account (Sandhya Suraksha reported at ₹1,200)
You do thisGovernment does this (you wait)Pension received
Before you apply
1
You
Find your pension ನಿಮ್ಮ ಪಿಂಚಣಿ ಯಾವುದು
  • Old-age pension (with central share) for poor seniors
  • Sandhya Suraksha for seniors from low-income working families
  • Widow pension for widows from low-income families
Ask the Nadakacheri which one fits your age and income.
2
You
Keep these papers ready ದಾಖಲೆಗಳನ್ನು ಸಿದ್ಧವಾಗಿಡಿ
  • Aadhaar and age proof
  • Income certificate (Nadakacheri)
  • Widows: husband's death certificate
  • Bank passbook (account linked to Aadhaar), photo
3
You
Apply ಅರ್ಜಿ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಿ
Apply on Seva Sindhu or at a Nadakacheri / Grama One centre. Keep the acknowledgement.
Tip: Applying is free. Do not pay anyone to 'sanction' a pension.
After you apply
4
Government
Verification ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆ
The Village Accountant and Revenue Inspector check age, income and family details.
Stuck? A 2025 review flagged many pensions as possibly ineligible. Keep your income certificate and age proof current so your pension is not stopped.
5
Government
Tahsildar sanctions ತಹಶೀಲ್ದಾರ್ ಮಂಜೂರಾತಿ
The Tahsildar sanctions the pension.
6
Done
Pension every month ಪ್ರತಿ ತಿಂಗಳು ಪಿಂಚಣಿ
The pension is paid into your account. Check that the amount matches the current rate.
Stuck? Payment stopped? Check Aadhaar seeding at the bank, then ask the taluk office whether your pension was suspended after review.
Stopped or not sanctioned? Complain.
Write to the Tahsildar with your acknowledgement number, or complain on IPGRS. Ask for the reason in writing if it was stopped.
Apply: sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in, Nadakacheri, Grama One · Amounts change; check the current rate at the taluk office
Applying is free · Use only official websites
